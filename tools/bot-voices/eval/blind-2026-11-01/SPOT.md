# Spot-the-bot judgement (180 lines)

Calls: 76 bot, 104 player. Confidence: bot 29 sure / 25 likely / 22 guess; player 1 sure / 49 likely / 54 guess.

## Tells relied on most (ranked by how decisive they were)

1. **Narrated lore and observational aphorisms (22 bot calls, mostly confidence 3).** These are third-person "flavor" musings that nobody types mid-session in 2002-03 chat: they comment on the game instead of playing it.
   Examples: #16 "uther's shade haunts stonehenge barrows...", #20 "the journal fell out of the sky at prydwen keep...", #57 "the bind stone and i are on first name terms", #73 "the vale of mularn is where most mid first learned to swing", #171 "a purple con walking past the group makes everyone go very quiet".
2. **Duplicates and near-duplicates (4).** #67 and #144 ("give me camelot hills over every/any hib forest"), #90 and #145 ("Ha yes, this exactly." / "Heh yep, this exactly."). Lines that differ by one swapped word come from a template.
3. **Template leaks and slot-grammar glitches (4, all confidence 3).** #31 "[JoinReply]", #79 "[where the bot is]", #95 "a kobold with a grudge are worse...", #168 "...exactly like a stump, any alb who learned the hard way".
4. **Polished capitalization and punctuation, including roleplay prose (13).** Real chat at the time was lowercase with no full stop. Examples: #3 "Np, have fun.", #70 "Thanks to everyone who went.", #136 "Lol yes.", #7 "The Morrigan watches over us tonight.", #154 "Brothers and sisters, we march at dusk...".
5. **Generic filler acks that fit any context (23, mostly confidence 1-2).** These reply to nothing in particular: "good question", "happy to help", "you and me both", "sounds familiar", "mhm. anyway, back to it", "just thinking out loud".
6. **Smaller tells:** LFG lines with a canned tail (#55 "lf grp 9 warden, been playing a while", #87 "...not logging anytime soon"); plugs for the server's custom exchange (#62, #75); world-state lines worded like a news ticker (#12 "Caer Benowyc is gone, Midgard has it now"); a fact wrong for the era (#10 "lvl 20 epic armor").

**Player signals:** urgent combat callouts with no polish ("stop casting on the mezzed one!!!", "inc at bled!!", "ADD!"), period shorthand (gz gz, ding 1/2, lo all, kk, rr2, sb), specific newbie questions, situational detail ("got ganked 3 times leaving the portal keep"), and real-life asides ("re. kids are in bed finally", "back from dinner", "did u guys see the pic on the herald?").

## Hardest to call

- **Very short acks and replies** (lol, k, np, anytime, hail, ok ok, where?, Which keep?). Both humans and bots produce them, so these were coin flips. About half the confidence-1 calls fall here. I leaned "bot" for bland politeness and "player" for terse or lowercase forms.
- **Plain LFG and trade lines** ("lfg 41 wizard dungeon or camp dont mind", "20 shaman lfg, lets get a group going", "looking for group, lvl 12 mercenary"). Templates copy this format well.
- **Light personal humor** that could be a human or a persona generator (#148 "i fell off the bridge in tir na nog. again.", #175, #170).
- **Lines that name another character** (#84, #107, #135, #155). These are convincing either way, so I split them by how polished they were.
