section: Bots and companions
---
- **Gamebots no longer crowd the NPCs you click** (#569).
  - A gamebot party that meets at a merchant, trainer, stable master, healer or vault keeper now gathers 300-450 units
    beside it, clear of any other such NPC nearby and inside the town, instead of on the NPC's own spot. Two parties at
    one NPC take different spots. Where no clear spot fits, the party meets where it did before.
  - Gamebots arriving by stable master route each step off to their own spot 60-140 units from the route end, on the
    side away from the stable master, instead of all standing on the last point.
  - Town breaks pick a spot at least 250 units from those NPCs.
