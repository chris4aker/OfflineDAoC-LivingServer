- **Frontier keeps and towers have patrols** (#577; owner decision 2026-11-26). Two guards patrol each keep, three above
  keep level 4, and one each tower, walking a loop close to the walls, as on the New Frontiers live servers. The paths are
  DOL's public database's (Eve-of-Darkness/db-public, MIT). A world from an earlier build gets them once at start-up; a
  world that already has keep patrols of its own is left as it is.
