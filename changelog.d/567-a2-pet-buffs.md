section: Bots and companions
---
- **No wasted buffs on a bot's own pet** (#567 A2). A pet owner
  bot no longer keeps power regen, endurance regen, acuity or fatigue buffs on its own pet, which a pet can't use; party
  pets already had this rule (#491), and fatigue buffs are now on the list for both.
