- **Lone gamebots stop pacing back and forth in front of the same enemies** (#766). A solo or duo gamebot that backed away from a stronger force used to walk off, wait, head back along its way and back away again from the same group every few minutes, mostly in the battlegrounds. Now, when it meets the same group in the same place again within five minutes, it stops and keeps its distance where it stands. It doesn't run off again or call its friends again. A bigger group or a fresh one somewhere else still sends it back as before, and it still fights back when attacked.
