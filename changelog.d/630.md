- **New players join, and veterans retire** (#630; owner decision 2026-11-25, option 3, the interim step before the alt
  roster in #631). When a realm's gamebots in levels 1-19 fall below a floor (50 a realm at a roster of 1,000, scaled to
  the realm's roster), new level-1 gamebots join it, at most 6 an hour a realm (scaled the same way), made like the
  launcher's "+ Lv.1" batch: the realm's 2003 class mix, never the custom class, no armour, a Classic or Shrouded Isles
  start. Each newcomer is paired with a level-50 gamebot of its realm that retires (the longest idle first, then the
  oldest character), so the roster stays the same size. A retired bot keeps its character, items and coin, is never
  deleted and never logs in again; the launcher's bot list shows it as "Retired". Never retired: a bot guild leader, a
  bot in your guild or on your friends list, a bot in a group or raid, trading, with an open craft order, busy or in your
  sight. Settings `population_newcomers`, `population_newcomer_floor`, `population_newcomer_per_hour` and
  `population_retire`, on by default. Log rows `POPULATION_NEWCOMER` and `POPULATION_RETIRED`, with no names.
  Autonomous gamebots only; companions and the player are unchanged (`UT_NewcomerStream630`). **Tests only.**
