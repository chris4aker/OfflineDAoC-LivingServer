- **Gamebot party healers walk to a fallen member, but not into what killed them** (#570). Out of combat, one healer of
  a gamebot party walks to a member who died out of spell range and raises them, also while the party regroups. It
  stays away when the body lies beside a monster much stronger than the healer that is hunting or fighting, or at a
  place where such monsters have been killing bots of its level. While the healer is on its way, the party's dead wait
  for it for up to two minutes instead of releasing.
