- Pulling a single keep guard on your own no longer calls out the realm's defense. The alarm goes up once you kill a guard, hit a door, or come with others. The war horn and the "defense force has been rallied" line still sound when it does. (#523)
- When you take a keep, the realm that lost it now comes back to retake it only some of the time, not after every capture. (#523)
- When you attack a keep, the third realm no longer joins the fight at once. Sometimes it comes, a few minutes in, and sometimes it stays away, the same as in the bots' own sieges. (#523)
