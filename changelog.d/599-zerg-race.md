- **A zerg no longer pulls a group out of a keep defence** (#599 step 3 follow-up). When a group of a realm's zerg was
  called to defend a keep in the few seconds before the zerg went into its own attack, the zerg took it back and left the
  defence a group short. Now the group stays in the defence and leaves the zerg. A group below level 50 no longer goes
  with the zerg into a siege, as with any warband. Groups that leave the zerg this way carry on as warbands for a while.
  Autonomous gamebots only.
