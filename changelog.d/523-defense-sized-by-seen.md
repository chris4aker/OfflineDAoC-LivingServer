- When you attack a keep, the defense its realm sends now matches the attackers at the keep: you and the members of your group or companion raid who are actually there. Companions left behind, dead or across the zone no longer count, so a small strike party draws a small answer. As more of your force arrives the defense grows to meet it, and it never shrinks once rallied. (#523)
