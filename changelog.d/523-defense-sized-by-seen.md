- When you attack a keep, the defense its realm sends now matches the attackers at the keep: you and the members of your group or companion raid who are actually there, plus your realm's gamebots at that keep. The helpers that defense brings in from your own realm don't count toward its size, so attacking alone keeps drawing a small answer. Companions left behind, dead or across the zone no longer count, so a small strike party draws a small answer. As more of your force arrives the defense grows to meet it, and it never shrinks once rallied. (#523)
