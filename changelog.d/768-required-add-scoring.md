- **A lost fight on Myrddraxis's heads now counts** (#768; owner, 2026-11-27). Bosses whose adds must die first, in order
  (Myrddraxis's heads, Aroon's Scaiths, Xaga's Beatha and Tine, Scurceol Hyrde's orbs), are judged on those adds too, with
  the same rule as the boss himself: worn below 70% and back to full is a reset or a wipe. A raid beaten on the heads
  now uses one of its two tries and calls the Special Forces, instead of pulling again until its four hours run out. No
  boss changed.
