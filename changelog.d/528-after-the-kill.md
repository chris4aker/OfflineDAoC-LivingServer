- **After the kill** (#528). A stealther, soloer or roaming eight that kills you at a milegate or check spot now moves on
  within the move-on time, as it already did after killing a gamebot, instead of camping your corpse for the rest of its
  stay. Frontier inc calls name the realm really sieging the keep and a count close to the real one, and only a bot near
  the keep gives them; the rest of the realm just calls the keep under attack. Now and then one of your killers laughs,
  makes a rude gesture or taunts over your corpse, and bows after a fair one-on-one, never twice over the same body. The
  server log gets an hourly `RVR_RANKS` line with each realm's gamebots by realm rank.
