- **A Hibernian Bard can be a bot party's healer** (#567 B7; owner decision 2026-11-26; adapted from OfflineDAoC-True165
  BardGroupHealer165, b45daa9). When no Druid or Warden is in reach, a Bard may take a party's healer place and heals for
  it with its own Regrowth heals while it stays in that party; a Druid or Warden in reach always takes the place first.
  This is for ordinary groups only: realm raid parties are made up as before.
  Parties that waited with no healer at all can now form. One server setting, `bot_bard_fills_healer_slot` (on), turns
  it off.
