section: Bots and companions
---
- **Gamebot parties no longer stop and turn to buff while they walk** (#567 A1).
  A party of gamebots on the move kept stopping a member for a buff it was then not allowed to cast, or walking it toward
  a groupmate out of range until the formation pulled it back. Their ordinary buffs now wait until the party has stood
  still a moment; heals, fights, songs and travel speed are never held.
