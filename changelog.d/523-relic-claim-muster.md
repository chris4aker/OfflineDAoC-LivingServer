- **A relic on the road no longer stops the war everywhere** (#523 step 1 (f)). While a relic is being carried, gamebot armies don't open new sieges in that frontier, but the other frontiers keep fighting. Before, every new siege on the server waited for the relic.
- **The army that takes a keep stays to claim it** (#523 step 1 (h)). Gamebots that capture a keep hold it for three minutes to rez, regroup and plant their guild's banner before they move on. For the claim, the realm's guild members inside the keep count together, whatever their guild, the way a player's group counts.
- **Siege calls say where to gather** (#523 step 1 (i)): "Warbands are gathering at the portal outpost in Hadrian's Wall to assault Caer Benowyc. Join them there!" The retake call names its rally point too, and bots repeat the place in their realm chat.
