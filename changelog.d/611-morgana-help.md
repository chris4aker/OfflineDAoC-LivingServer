section: Bots and companions
---
- **Bots help with Morgana's quest fight** (#611; owner decision 2026-11-25). When your Albion level 50 epic event starts
  at the fallen tower in Lyonesse, your companions and the gamebots grouped with you go for her two demons, Bechard and
  Silcharde, starting with the nearer one. Healers stay back and heal, and a defensive companion keeps close to you.
  Bots never attack Morgana. Only a player on the quest can start the event, and Morgana and her demons are unchanged.
  One server setting, `bot_morgana_quest_help` (on), turns it off.
