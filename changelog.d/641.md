---
- **The name is now DAoC - Living Server** (#641; owner, 2026-11-25). Setup, its Start menu folder and its shortcuts
  read "DAoC - Living Server". New installs go to `C:\Games\LivingServer` by default. Existing installs stay where they
  are and keep updating.
- **Faster base-game download** (#641). Setup's own downloader fetches the base game's parts in parallel over up to six
  connections, with byte ranges inside the large parts. A cut connection or a stopped Setup continues where it stopped.
  Every part is still checked against its SHA-256 and refused on a mismatch. Setup shows the combined speed, and its
  log records the MB/s. If the downloader stops, the original download script fetches what is missing, as before
  (`FastDownloadTests`). **Tests only.**
