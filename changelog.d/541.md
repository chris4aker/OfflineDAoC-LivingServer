section: Companion helpers

- **A /spawn helper no longer gets a second neck item** (#541; tests only). `SetJewelryROG` now skips a jewelry slot
  (the neck, cloak, waist or jewelry slot, or a full ring or bracer pair) that an earlier equip step already filled,
  so spawning a helper from the whisper menu stops logging `Inventory.AddItem -> Destination slot is not empty (29)`.
  `UT_CompanionEquipmentDispatch.JewelryNeverAddsIntoAFilledSlot`.
