section: Bosses

- Raiders who can't find a safe way around the boss to their staging post now try the raid's other posts. After about
  a minute, they walk in the ordinary way when the boss leaves passers-by alone, like the Glacier Giant. Before this
  fix, they stood and retried for 20 minutes until the rally failed (#767).
- A raider stuck on the way to a rally is no longer exempt from the stuck check. After 15 minutes without moving, it
  is recovered like any stuck bot and keeps its place in the raid.
