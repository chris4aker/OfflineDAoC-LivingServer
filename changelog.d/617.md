- **Battleground fights: people before engines, the rally reacts as one, mezzes held** (#617; FarmVibes' crowd-control
  finding on #600 and review on #621). Autonomous gamebots in a battleground only; field RvR, companions and the player
  are unchanged. Enemy players, gamebots and pets rank above siege engines in the battleground scan (an engine only when
  no enemy person is left); a bot already hitting an engine switches to an enemy person in reach, and an attacking
  siege crew leaves its engine for an enemy person within 1,000 (`AutonomousBattlegroundTargets.SiegeCrewReleases`,
  beside `SiegeOperatorMayEngage`). A bot holding at the rally that sees an enemy heading for it, or fighting a
  realm-mate near it, posts the contact, and every bot at that rally, looker or not, takes it on its next scan
  (`AutonomousBattlegroundRallyContact`). No maintenance or self-buff pulse while an enemy the bot may fight is in sight;
  a gatherer with distant defenders in sight still buffs. The scan's picks and the controller's keep-assault,
  gate-clear and lord picks pass over a mezzed or breakably rooted enemy (roots at Value >= 99) while a free one is in
  the fight and take the free one instead, never the gate. `RvrTacticsScope` and the `RVR_BOT_*` switches are
  unchanged. `UT_BattlegroundFights617`. **Tests only.**
