# NPC pass: a sample for the owner (phase 1, no lore edited)

What phase 2 would do, shown on real passages: a generic role ("the smith", "the druids of Tir na Nog") becomes a named
present-day NPC from `tools/lore/npc_roster.json`, chosen by place and role (`tools/lore/npc_role_map.json`), within the
cast sheets (CAST-ALBION.md, CAST-MIDGARD.md, CAST-HIBERNIA.md, CAST-NEUTRAL.md) and the voice guides (VOICE-*.md).

The rule for every rewrite (owner, 2026-11-22: "phenomenal and tight"): the name **replaces** the vague phrase. Nothing
old is cut, and the sentence should come out the same length or shorter. Word counts are given for each pair.

## The 10 strongest and 5 weakest pages, by realm

My judgement, after reading the stories, chapters and book pages. "Weakest" here means the page's visible text reads as
a guide or a directory, or breaks the era; their stories are often good. The weak lines are mostly in `legend` (what the
raid leaders say), where the owner allows the raiders' words of 2003 (decision 9): the fault is the walkthrough form,
not the words.

### Albion

Strongest:
1. `bosses/golestandt`: "Albion learned her name exactly once, and paid thirty knights for it"; the audit's best, still.
2. `locations/krondon`: a hall that lived by titles over its doors, and two older words cut under them.
3. `locations/avalon-city`: Elowen's list of the colleges carries a city's whole history.
4. `locations/hall-of-the-corrupt`: Vibius's tablet and its rule, "never answer a question asked from below".
5. `bosses/captain-heathyr`: the joiner's daughter who rehung the last door; a present-day life in one trade.
6. `locations/caer-sidi`: Arthur's seven and the third company's sixty-seven cut together over one door.
7. `bosses/xanxicar`: the Quieting, Sir Garan's two hundred, and the light on the mound no one has touched.
8. `bosses/orylle`: she took the hall in one night without breaking a door.
9. `locations/crystal-cave`: Iorwerth in the deepest cellar, and the vein he never passed.
10. `bosses/apocalypse`: "WE WERE NOT HIS CONQUERORS", cut with a borrowed chisel.

Weakest:
1. `locations/snowdonia` (legend): a directory of NPCs in brackets ("lots of quests").
2. `bosses/adelon` (legend): "The quest runs: ..." step by step.
3. `bosses/veena` (legend): two quest titles with their level bands in brackets.
4. `locations/isle-of-glass` (legend): "the three forts ... have quests and merchants".
5. `locations/albion-housing` (story): "a Market Explorer" in every village; the Market Explorer came after 1.65.

### Midgard
