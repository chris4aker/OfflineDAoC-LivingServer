# How the shared places sound (voice guide for the NPC pass)

Darkness Falls, the battlegrounds, the Proving Fields and the Shrouded Isles umbrella.

## Rule 1: phenomenal and tight

Owner, 2026-11-22: "Stories should be phenomenal and tight", "don't be long for the sake of length".

- Every sentence adds a fact, an image or a consequence. A sentence that only restates, frames or announces goes.
- Nothing old is cut (decision 3). Tighten by rewording, never by dropping an event or a detail.
- A new name replaces a vague phrase; it does not sit beside it. A name should usually leave the sentence the same length
  or shorter.

## Two voices, kept apart

**The court below** speaks in the voice of its own records: the Rolls, the Archivist's record room, the Chancellor's
precedence. Formal, legal, cold, and funny by accident: a court that eats a lord who forgets his place. Its dates are
Rolls and Ages ("In the Sixth Roll"), never years.

**The realms above** speak in their own voices (VOICE-ALBION, VOICE-MIDGARD, VOICE-HIBERNIA) when they come down. A
Darkness Falls page is strongest when the three realms' records sit side by side and disagree.

- **Forms:** the court's Roll entry, the Door-Book (Albion's record of the door's openings), the capitals' rolls of the
  first breakers, the imp's bargain, the chaplain's gate book at Abermenai.
- **Rhythm:** the court's long periodic sentence with its rank in order, then a realm's plain one beside it.

## Model passages

1. `history/neutral`, "The Feast of Thrones":
   > Twenty-nine came armed. The thirtieth came hungry.

   The whole making of Legion in two sentences.
2. `locations/darkness-falls`, book:
   > The imp called itself Pico, and said it kept a stall in the Albion hall. I set its name down so that the next reader
   > may ask for it, and bring candle-ends.

   A present-day figure named for a reason, and a consequence for the next reader.
3. `bosses/prince-asmoien`, from the Door-Book:
   > He wrote in the Door-Book that the Free Company was disbanded the night it came home, because it had never been his
   > to keep.

   A realm's company, its record and its cost in one sentence.

## Passages to avoid

1. `bosses/director-kobil`, legend:
   > He rises again in about five minutes, so get the quest done and move on to Abdin; he is more annoying than dangerous.

   Allowed in fight advice (owner, 2026-11-22), but flat. In-world: why the court lets its director rise so soon.
2. `bosses/ambassador-mannam`, legend:
   > He is a kill in every realm's Strange Bedfellows quest: Mystemas sends you after Nahemah and Mannam, ...

   A quest summary where the court's own reason (Mannam's embassy to Albion's throne) is already in the story.
