# Era research: how people played open-field RvR at 1.65 (Oct 2003)

Researched 2026-11-16 for the bot open-field design. Tags: **(era-dated)** = written 2002 to Jan 2004; **(retrospective)** = written later about that era, mostly Uthgard (1.65 freeshard) players.

How to read the sources:
- The FreddysHouse (FH) threads are European live servers. A poster in Aug 2003 says "post-1.62", so they sit at or just under 1.65. I read them in full.
- Uthgard threads are 2009-2018 recollection or freeshard practice. They are polluted by later rules. **Do not use from them:** RR5 abilities, Charge, New Frontiers/Agramon, anything "on live".
- Patch-note items marked "wiki summary" came through a page summariser, not a verbatim read. Check the wording before hard-coding numbers.

## 1. The 8-man: compositions and roles

- Midgard, mid-2003: "a fg mids who have 2-3 healers, a shammy and for the rest tanks" was the group others found hardest. ([FH Aug 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/what-is-a-good-tactic-to.140721/), era-dated)
- The stacked formations named at the time: "3 Healers or 3 Savages in Mid, 3 Chanters in Hib, 3 Mercs in Alb". (same thread, era-dated)
- Midgard, a guild's actual 1.65 group: Healer, Healer, Shaman, Runemaster, Skald/Savage, Savage, Berserker, Warrior. ([Uthgard 2016](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=61&t=37247), retrospective)
- Hibernia, "the popular Hib group": 2 Druid, Bard, Warden, 2 PBAoE casters, 2 tanks. ([FH Aug 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/what-is-a-good-tactic-to.140721/), era-dated)
- Hibernia tank group: "Bard, Druid, Druid, Warden, 3 BMs, 1 Hero"; the "classic fotm" was 2 Bard, 2 Druid, 2 BM, 2 Hero, the second Bard for speed 6 and endurance. ([FH Jan 2004](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/best-hib-tank-group.167967/), era-dated)
- Hibernia caster group: 3-4 Enchanters "box very well" behind a slam tank; "he slams a savage, you pbaoe it dead". ([FH Aug 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/what-is-a-good-tactic-to.140721/), era-dated)
- Albion: support of "2clerics and 1 sorc", tanks as the killers, opening with Speed of Sound, surviving on two Bunker of Faith. (same thread, era-dated)
- Albion full list: Cleric, Cleric, Sorc, Theurgist, Paladin, Minstrel, Merc, Merc. ([Uthgard guide 2011](https://uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=65&t=22425), retrospective)
- Role skeleton for every realm: two primary healers, one primary crowd controller, one defensive (guard/slam) tank, a speed class, two demezzers; plus Shaman (Mid) or Theurgist (Alb). (same guide, retrospective)
- Speed class: groups "practically required" one, to close on casters and escape zergs. ([Qt3 2013](https://forum.quartertothree.com/t/realm-vs-realm-in-guild-wars-2-and-dark-age-of-camelot/71405), [Uthgard 2016](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=61&t=37247), retrospective)

## 2. The assist train

- Method: "Nominate a tank from your group as the main assist. Everyone then makes an /assist macro... when he has a target hit your assist macro then wade in". ([FH Nov 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/assist.160018/), era-dated)
- "even if MA is not leading ALL tanks stick to the ma". A melee player spams the assist macro until a target appears, then sticks and swings. ([FH Aug 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/feds-rvr-tips.144566/), era-dated)
- Kill order as taught in Jan 2003: casters, healers, PBT casters, CC classes, tanks last. Short form: "anything that wears a dress and moves their arms about". ([FH Jan 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/rvr-tips-guide.108888/), era-dated)
- Versus Midgard: "mezz the healers, kill the shaman and then kill the tanks"; "kill those who carry a small shield". ([FH Aug 2003 a](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/what-is-a-good-tactic-to.140721/), [b](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/feds-rvr-tips.144566/), era-dated)
- Versus Hibernia: the Bard first (speed, endurance, main CC), then Eldritch, then Druids. ([Uthgard 2011](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=60&t=24492), retrospective)
- Switch triggers: "If you see a caster use MoC kill it and kill it fast"; re-kill whoever was just instantly resurrected. (FH Aug 2003 a/b, era-dated)
- Assist was not universal. Against three-healer Hib groups: "leave the MA and go hit em"; "skilled players should know when to /assist and when not to". One poster proposes splitting the tanks in two squads. (same, era-dated)
- Speed of a train: a Bard describes an "MA raping me with his assist train in 2 sec". ([FH Jan 2004](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/best-hib-tank-group.167967/), era-dated)
- No source measures how fast the train swaps target. See section 9.

## 3. Crowd control order

- Immunity: a mez/stun/root can land again "1 minute after their previous mez/stun/root has worn off", RvR and guards only. ([Mythic producer note, Jan 2002](https://dolserver.sourceforge.net/viewtopic.php?p=133788), era-dated)
- AE mez lasts full time at the centre and 50% at the edge; cure-mez spells were added for several casters. ([1.52 notes, wiki summary](https://camelotherald.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_Notes:_Version_1.52), era-dated)
- Resists shorten mez/root/snare/stun and never lengthen them. ([1.50 notes, wiki summary](https://camelotherald.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_Notes:_Version_1.50), era-dated)
- Determination cuts duration 15% per level, so tanks talk of "the 5 sec mezz". ([Old RA list](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=20197), retrospective data; [FH Aug 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/what-is-a-good-tactic-to.140721/), era-dated)
- The opener is AE mez on the charge. The answer is to spread: "Learn to scatter as soon as you see any incomming forces", run at the target "in an arc". ([FH Aug 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/feds-rvr-tips.144566/), era-dated)
- Instant mez/stun needs facing: Bards and Healers "can't insta mez (stun) you if you're hitting them from behind". (same, era-dated)
- Break a mez only with a stun (caster stun or slam). "DEMEZZ!!!!" was the standing complaint. If a mez is resisted, cast it again. (FH Jan and Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Purge: clears everything, 30-minute reuse, no delay listed. Group Purge and Perfect Recovery also 30 minutes. ([Old RA list](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=20197), retrospective data) Era advice: do not waste Purge on a mez unless you are a healer, mezzer or stunner. (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Good versus average: good groups call the mez, hold damage and demez; average ones "use AEO to break mezz". (FH Jan 2003 satire, era-dated)

## 4. Interrupts

- A hit, a miss and a hit absorbed by a bladeturn all interrupt the caster; a hit on the caster's pet does not. ([Uthgard live test 2009](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=14&t=12010), retrospective)
- From 1.55 a successful Guard or Intercept no longer interrupts the guarded caster. (1.55 note quoted in the same thread; anticipated in [FH Jan 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/rvr-tips-guide.108888/), era-dated)
- **Post-1.65, do not use:** the fixed 2-second spell interrupt is a 1.68 change. The 1.65 duration is unconfirmed.
- Mastery of Concentration: no interruption for 15 seconds, 30-minute reuse (as listed). ([Old RA list](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=20197), retrospective data)
- Who interrupts whom: Minstrel is "Front Line Rupt"; Theurgist pets go on casters and support; pets take out a kiting Shaman. ([Uthgard 2016](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?p=355908), [Uthgard 2011](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=60&t=24492), retrospective)
- Champion (DD and snare) and Warden were valued as interrupters in Hib tank groups. (FH Jan 2004, era-dated)
- Rule: "If a healer purges then keep it interrupted somehow." (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Archers expected their job to be "targetting and taking out the casters". ([Ranger guide, May 2002](https://camelot.allakhazam.com/Ranger_Guide.html), era-dated)
- I found no era source for a formal "interrupt rotation".

## 5. Peeling and protection

- "Shield tanks - Guard + intercept + /stick on your casters, slam meleers that get too close." ([FH Jan 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/rvr-tips-guide.108888/), era-dated)
- "Try to snare/slam/stun/mez people on your healers/nukers/tanks." (same, era-dated)
- "if a savage ever attacks u stop whatever it is ur doing and stun it". (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Defence resolves evade, parry, shield, guard, hit/miss, bladeturn. ([Grab Bag, Mar 2002](https://dolserver.sourceforge.net/viewtopic.php?p=133902), era-dated) Protect is "an always-on taunt, designed to save only one person". ([Hero guide, undated](https://camelot.allakhazam.com/Hero_Guide.html), era-dated, date uncertain)
- Healers kite: "run a bit, throw heal". Never turn your back on a Berserker or Savage; circle-strafe and /face so positional styles fail. (FH Jan and Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Stun a healer when it is low, not at the start, to deny instant heals. (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Casters: do not face the target squarely; cast after /face while moving. (same, era-dated)
- Keep more than 350 units apart; two healers closer than that invite mez and root. ([Uthgard guide 2011](https://uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=65&t=22425), retrospective)

## 6. Movement

- Speed of Sound: group at twice normal speed for 30 seconds, not broken by combat, ignores mez/root/snare for movement. ([Old RA list](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=20197), retrospective data) Albion opened with it. (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- "Speed 5" and "speed6" were era terms. (FH Aug 2003, Jan 2004, era-dated)
- Stick discipline: "if he says stick, STICK, even if there is one more person to kill". (FH Jan 2003, era-dated)
- Some stuck "the person above you in the mini group window" so the group ran strung out. Unstick when resting and "keep spinning around". (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- After a fight: stick, leave, rest elsewhere. "The place you just fought will be the first place the enemy come looking." Never rest at AMG or MMG. (FH Jan 2003, era-dated)
- Hibernia's run: buff at Druim Ligen, race to Crim/Bolg, meet a second group, rebuff, get a report from Emain, then go. (same, era-dated)
- Chasing: "Do not chase enemy to Alb TK... you WILL die". Never chase a grouped Hib into the PBAoE. (FH Jan and Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Outnumbered: "kill and then run like hell to somewhere else". Do not run so far you lose your group. (same, era-dated)
- Group-level calls: push casters and Albs, kite zergs and tank groups, break off when a member cannot follow. ([Uthgard guide 2011](https://uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=65&t=22425), retrospective)
- Rez: "Don't leave dead realm mates behind, rez and then move on". Low-health "ghetto-ress" needs a follow-up heal. Three Healers with Perfect Recovery meant "11 players rather than just 8". (FH Jan and Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Release when rezzing in a camped spot turns into "a vicious circle". Portal keeps had bindstones from 1.52; a death cost "5-10 minutes". (FH Jan 2003; 1.52 notes, era-dated; Qt3 2013, retrospective)

## 7. Zergs, 8-mans, small-man, stealth

- Sizes: Hibs hit Benowyc "with 50" and Crauchon with 3 fg at once; "4fg" in Emain was normal; "groups of upto 20 albs". ([FH Jul 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/the-battle-of-benowyc.135669/), [FH Sep 2002](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/darkness-falls.97652/), era-dated)
- Early peak recalled: three-way fights at AMG "with like 100 mids/alb 40 hibs". ([FH Apr 2004](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/good-old-days.15185/), retrospective)
- Zerg behaviour: "Zergs move slow and rarely venture too far from their spawn point; AMG for albs". The Alb zerg had little speed, fire AE everywhere, "incoming - and everyone did do something". ([Uthgard 2011](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=22640), [Uthgard 2018](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=47221), retrospective)
- Zergs fed the 8-mans, and average groups won when "3fg+ add onto the fight". (Uthgard 2018, retrospective; [FH Nov 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/amg-tonight.157865/), era-dated)
- Stealthers camped the milegates in packs: "3 fg Mids, and the required 1 fg hunters and SB were camping amg". "almost everyone" did it. (FH Jul and Nov 2003, era-dated)
- Stealth groups ran 3-8, partly to counter See Hidden. Visible soloing was near-impossible: "invisible or join a zerg". ([Uthgard 2009](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=12717), [Uthgard 2011](https://uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=22430), retrospective)
- No source gives proportions of zerg, 8-man and solo play.

## 8. Where people fought

- Emain Macha held the bulk of it ("80%"; "AMG AMG AMG all the time"). Reasons: few aggressive mobs, Albs and Mids teleport in, Hibs walk. (Qt3 2013; [Uthgard 2017](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=43212); Uthgard 2018, retrospective)
- Emain place names: ATK/APK and MTK/MPK (teleport keeps), AMG, MMG, "cruach" (Crauchon merchant keep), Levi ridge, Dhak's Tower by AMG, Grogans plain near Crauchon, Grannies "on the right when you move through AMG", Middle Hill, valley 1 (Crim side), valley 2 (Bolg side). ([FH Jan 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/rvr-tips-guide.108888/), era-dated)
- The lane: "a long Road headed from crim -> amg + mmg"; groups ran "amg -> mmg and back all night"; infiltrators camped MPK. ([Uthgard 2009](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=10985), retrospective; FH Nov 2003 and [Aug 2002](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/gg-in-df.96112/), era-dated)
- Quieter options: Breifine and around Druim Ligen for group fights; MMG, Sauvage and Uppland for solo. Chain order Emain to Breifine to Gorge. (Uthgard 2011 and 2017, retrospective)
- Odin's Gate, Jamtland, Uppland: Mid foot traffic to the relic keep and Dodens Gruva; gankers outside Svasud. Hadrian's, Pennine, Sauvage: solos, small groups, "lots of people looking to gank lvlers at cyraeths/giants". (Uthgard 2017, retrospective)
- Yggdra "was THE greyganking area... the only frontier zone where people actually exped alot". (FH Aug 2002, era-dated)
- Midgard frontier camps: crab plateau behind Svasud, Bone Eater Oracle camp north of Grallarhorn Faste, Blendrake wraiths, Arvakr hills, Nottmoor liches, Glenlock rock giants. ([Uthgard a](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=61&t=27427), [b](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=61&t=44021), retrospective)
- Albion: hill giants and trees NW of Castle Sauvage, Arawnites in Snowdonia, fellwoods/cliff crawlers/angry bwca in Pennine, boogie men behind APK in Emain. ([Uthgard](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=60&t=28529), retrospective)
- Hibernia: sett dwellers in Cruachan Gorge and Mount Collory, corybantic skeletons by the strength relic keep, Marfach Caverns (40-50, entrance in Breifine). ([Uthgard](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=62&t=27426), retrospective; [Allakhazam](https://camelot.allakhazam.com/db/zones.html?czone=70), era-dated database)
- Darkness Falls: "Whoever has the most keeps finds their portal open." Mids retook a keep "within 30 mins". People levelled there and were killed there by enemy realms. ([Apr 2002 news](https://gamebanshee.com/3d4r); [FH Sep 2003](https://forums.freddyshouse.com/threads/darkness-falls.145348/); FH Aug 2002, era-dated)

## 9. Tells of a human group

- Mistakes players mocked in their own realm: afk on stick, losing stick, healers charging, AE breaking mez, never turning the camera, half the group afk after a win, splitting two groups and dying one at a time, chasing into guards. (FH Jan 2003, era-dated)
- Disobedience and delay: the leader calls stick, the group "runs around a bit, climbing up wall", then is run over. (same, era-dated)
- Fixation: weaker tanks "keep trying to hit their back attack"; tanks "dont pan camera and overextend easily". (FH Aug 2003, era-dated; [Uthgard 2011](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=60&t=24492), retrospective)
- Hesitation and misreads: "If you see a fg approaching lead by a kobold don't jump the gun". Bards drew a weapon to look like tanks. (FH Aug 2003, era-dated)
- Emotes: "/laugh at the corpse". (Uthgard 2011, retrospective)
- Skill spread: "If you die without saying something it is usually your fault"; average groups box up, break mez and dump or hoard long timers. (Uthgard guide 2011, retrospective)
- Reaction time: average gamer about 300 ms, good 250, elite 150-200, non-gamer 350; a second choice adds about 50%, a third doubles it. A healer watching the right way starts healing "2 secs earlier". ([WPI 2020](https://web.cs.wpi.edu/~claypool/papers/reaction-time/paper.pdf), general study, not DAoC; FH Aug 2003, era-dated)

## 10. Rule constraints at 1.65

- 1.65 went live 8 Oct 2003. It removed Brilliant Aura of Deflection, Avoidance of Magic and Volcanic Pillar, and matched frontier-keep XP bonuses to dungeons. ([1.65 notes, wiki summary](https://camelotherald.fandom.com/wiki/Patch_Notes:_Version_1.65), era-dated)
- Old realm abilities in play: Purge, Group Purge, Ignore Pain, MoC, Speed of Sound, Bunker of Faith, Perfect Recovery, Determination, Raging Power, See Hidden, True Sight, Vanish; active ones mostly on 15 or 30 minutes. ([Old RA list](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=20197), retrospective data)
- Stealth: See Hidden lets assassins see archers and Minstrels; 1.62 cut that to about half range; True Sight is 60 seconds per 30 minutes; Camouflage counters it. No re-stealth for 10 seconds after a shot. ([Uthgard 2009](https://www.uthgard.net/forum/viewtopic.php?f=32&t=12717), retrospective; Ranger guide May 2002, era-dated)
- Keep and border-keep hasteners give a free speed buff; Hadrian's Wall and Odin's Gate mobs were made less aggressive. (1.52 notes, wiki summary, era-dated)

- Exact speed percentages (speed 5/6, sprint), sprint duration: not sourced.
- Interrupt duration at 1.65; whether style stuns share the 1-minute immunity: not sourced.
- MoC 15 seconds and Purge cost by class: one retrospective list only.
- Dodens Gruva and Hall of the Corrupt locations; portal keep proper names; DF entrance locations: not confirmed.
- Typical zerg size on a US server, shares of each playstyle, how fast real groups swapped target: no data found.

- Camelot Herald originals and RvR videos could not be opened; Herald text comes via reposts and a wiki.
