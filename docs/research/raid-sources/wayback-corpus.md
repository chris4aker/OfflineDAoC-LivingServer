# Wayback corpus: raid bosses and raid tactics (2001-2005)

What this is: every period fact about raid bosses and raid tactics found in the local Wayback scrape
`/home/claude/raid-research/wayback/` (pass of 2026-11-14). Everything is paraphrased; quotes are
kept to short phrases. Each fact carries a source key (see the Source key below) and a confidence:

- **H (high):** official Mythic text (Camelot Herald patch notes, Grab Bag), or two or more
  independent first-hand player sources that agree.
- **M (medium):** one detailed first-hand player account, or a map drawn by players in the period.
- **L (low):** rumour, second-hand, beta-era, contradicted elsewhere, or my own inference (marked
  "inference").

Period caveat: the epic encounters were changed several times (Galladoria made harder in Dec 2002;
epic-monster resists lowered around 1.60 in early 2003; Apocalypse toned down in 1.65 in autumn 2003;
"old world" dragons hardened against raids under 3 groups in 1.65). A tactic is only valid for the
date of its source. Dates are given for that reason.

## 1. Cross-cutting raid mechanics (apply to most bosses)

These matter more for bot raid AI than any single boss, because period players describe the same
engine rules again and again.

- **Attacker count lowers the boss's defence.** Each attacker, of any level, adds to a count that
  makes the target easier to hit. Bosses whose level is set very high (Beliathan before his level
  cut, Apocalypse) are near-unhittable for small forces and easy once enough bodies are on them;
  pet spam (theurgist pets) works because pets count. S1 (M). Same idea in S10 for King Tuscar and
  Queen Kula ("pets count as added attackers" which lower their huge AF) (M). Majestic Will was
  granting far more than its documented 15% to-hit and was cut in 1.68b because epic monsters and
  dragons were dying faster and faster: H1356, 26 Feb 2004 (H).
- **Relog pulling.** A puller who has left the group and relogged (or died and was rezzed without a
  heal) pulls single targets even from bring-a-friend packs. Healing the puller, grouping him or
  attacking at once re-links the pack. Raids kept a dedicated full-rez healer for the puller. S1 (M);
  S10 adds: pull with something that does no damage (shaman disease or a level-1 bolt, runemaster or
  healer nearsight/amnesia at about 2300 range), and never heal the puller while the raid fights (H,
  two independent sources).
- **Stand behind parrying bosses.** Bellum, Wrath of Mordred and the Grand Summoner parry a lot from
  the front. S1 (M); S4 says the same of the Geomancer (M).
- **Aggro group.** Suggested Albion tank group: defensive paladin as main tank, a guarding armsman,
  two earth theurgists stacking point-blank ablatives, a minstrel, three rejuvenation clerics. Let
  this group hit for a few seconds before the raid. S1 (M).
- **Resurrection group.** A non-fighting group of rez clerics plus a minstrel or sorcerer for power
  (necromancer power transfer also used). Not recommended at Apocalypse, where every attacker counts.
  S1 (M).
- **Debuffs.** AF debuffs especially, because hit rate and damage are low on epics. S1 (M).
- **Dragons hardened against tiny raids.** All "old world" dragons became more resistant to attacks
  from raids of fewer than 3 groups; Mythic said fighting behaviour was unchanged, only the minimum
  head count rose. H1082 (29 Sep 2003) and H1095 (1.65 live) (H). This came right after the
  15-25-person dragon raids of Aug 2003 (S7, S38).
- **Epic resists.** A bug that raised direct-damage resist rates on epic monsters (level 65+) was
  fixed in 1.65D, 15 Sep 2003: H1056 (H). Salaris notes an earlier general cut to epic monster
  resistances before Feb 2003 (S1, M; exact patch not in corpus).
- **Pets cannot be used from outside an engage radius.** From 1.70 (22 Jun 2004) pets can't attack a
  target that has an engage radius while their owner stands outside it; this explicitly covers
  dragons, keep lords and tower captains. H1549 (H).
- **Confused monsters steal kill credit.** A confused mob that lands the killing blow can rob a raid
  of encounter credit; Mythic said this happened a lot on dragon raids. H1676 (H).
- **Spawn timers are probabilistic.** All spawns, dragons included, roll a percentage chance every
  few minutes; nothing spawns instantly after a patch. H1125 (H). Players measured the dragon on a
  30-minute roll with a small chance each time (one Merlin raid saw three pops in about two hours).
  S7 (M). "Approx 1-24 hours" from another player, S7 (L).
- **Battlegroup credit.** From 1.66h (13 Nov 2003) Legion, Green Maw, the Ellyl seer, Abomos the
  Soultrapper, Scurceol Hyrde and four Krondon worts give quest credit to the whole battlegroup.
  H1197 (H).
- **Epic dungeon rewards.** Jan 2003: more coin and higher drop rates for non-boss monsters in Caer
  Sidi, Tuscaran and Galladoria, aimed at smaller raids (patch text quoted in S5) (H).
  Each epic dungeon drops full and single-line respec stones (Classes of Camelot FAQ, `6-mechanics-20261113/www.classesofcamelot.com/06284dac67441f04_20050306215534.html`) (H).

## 2. Caer Sidi (Albion SI epic dungeon, Caldey)

Location: west coast of Caldey (S22 map) (H).

**General (S1 unless noted, M).**
- 15 epic bosses. The weakest "keylords" can be fought by about 3 full groups.
- The whole dungeon respawns at once, 8-10 hours after the first keylord dies, so the clear is a race.
  S37 (Excalibur EU, 2004) gives a fixed 10 hours from the first keylord kill and asks raids not to
  book within 10 hours of each other (M). Combined: H.
- Use 2-3 dedicated relog pullers working ahead of the raid.
- Each keylord drops one 100% quality item plus random drops; class armor from Caer Sidi completes a
  set that unlocks Apocalypse's necklaces (S1, S8) (M).
- Later: class armor added for Heretic, Bainshee, Vampiir, Warlock, Valkyrie (Herald more/1824, 2005)
  (H).

**Keylords, in Salaris' order** (S1, M; names as he wrote them, he admits shaky spelling):

| Boss | Mechanics | Raid size / tactic |
|---|---|---|
| Skeletal S. (Skeletal Sacristan; the loot list has a "Sacristan Coward's Belt", S8) | Calls for help and flees at high speed. | Clear his whole area first; pin him between two groups so he keeps turning and gets stuck. 2 groups min. |
| Spectral Provisioner | Fights back; drops items into players' backpacks to encumber them, then runs. | Watch encumbrance. 3+ groups. |
| Lich Lord Irion (Herald spells it **Ilron**) | Harder than the next several. Makes several copies of himself that all attack; the real one looks solid, the copies ghostly; killing the real one kills all. Casts area mez. | Hold the raid at the central staircase until pullers have brought in everything but the boss; assist the leader to pick the solid one. 4-5 groups. |
| Warlord D. | Straight melee, heavy damage, big HP, parries. Like a harder Wrath of Mordred. | Stand behind. |
| Silencer (ooze) | Adapts to damage types; whoever uses the adapted type is teleported into a nearby pit. Weak offense, high level. | Clear the pit first so teleportees survive. 4-5 groups. |
| Bane of Hope | Straight melee; when badly hurt teleports to another nearby spot and regenerates. | Pre-place players at the likely spots. 3-4 groups. |
| Lich Lord Sanguis | Starts as an Archwizard; on death adds spawn and he returns as the Lich Lord. Parries; weak to crush and thrust, strong vs slash and most magic. | 4+ groups. |
| Crypt Lord | Moderate melee; danger is the big add pack around him. | Clear the whole area before aggro. |
| Soul Reckoner | Reckoned Souls around him must die before he can be hurt. Seems to heal on player kills (or regenerates fast). | Clear area; kill souls first. |
| The Host | Several strong mobs that move together; killing any one kills them all. | Clear area first. |

Herald changes to Ilron, 1.66m, 28 Oct 2003 (H1145, H): his images no longer hit him; when he drops
his image spell he now broadcasts to everyone nearby; the delay between combat ending and the image
spell dropping was lengthened slightly (implies the images persist after combat for a while) (H).

**The Four Horsemen** (S1, M). Entering the room triggers a speech; answer their question and they
prepare. About one minute between horsemen, during which they are invulnerable. Bring at least 6
groups from the second one on. Respawn tied to the keylord clock.
- Fames (1st): plain melee, easiest.
- Bellum (2nd): instantly summons animated weapons that are very deadly; kill Bellum fast and the
  weapons vanish. Parries (stand behind).
- Morbus (3rd): creates adds that must all die before Morbus can be hurt; weaker than Bellum's.
- Funus (4th): only clerics can damage him; any other class's attack heals him to full.
- Each horseman drops two 100% items.

**Apocalypse** (S1, M unless noted).
- Called perhaps the hardest boss in the game. Very high level: about 100 attackers needed just to
  hit reliably; 70-100 minimum; Salaris' smallest live kill was 105 people.
- On pull he flies up and fire rains from the ceiling for the rest of the fight.
- After about four (Salaris unsure) player deaths, Harbingers of Fate start spawning, two at a time,
  four in total. Kill them before Apocalypse (less HP, nearly his damage).
- Holding him on one tank is possible but unlikely; keep maximum attackers on, debuffed, pet spammed.
- Anyone who moves too far away is teleported back to the centre of the room.
- No rez group: no recovering a failed attempt.
- Drops 3-4 100% items incl. Caer Sidi class breastplates, weapons, and necklaces usable only with a
  full Caer Sidi class suit (unlimited buff charges such as a big str/con buff or power regen) (S1, S8).
- Patch: "tweaked slightly downward" to match the other epic dungeon finals, 1.65 (H1082 29 Sep 2003;
  H1095) (H). Player memory (S30 comment, 2003): about 100 people still wiped (L).

**Contradictions:** Salaris calls the Lich Lord "Irion"; Herald says "Ilron" (trust Herald). Salaris
calls Apocalypse the hardest boss; a 2003 Allakhazam comment ranks the Hibernian dragon above him
(S30, L).

## 3. Tuscaran Glacier (Midgard SI epic dungeon)

## 4. Galladoria (Hibernian SI epic dungeon)

**Layout and boss key** (S11 map, M): entrance at top; numbered bosses with levels:
1 Conservator 77 (pool near the entrance), 2 Olcasar Geomancer 77 (north), 3 Organic Energy Machine
("the Mechanism") 79 (north-east wing), 4 Spindler Broodmother 81 (west ring), 5 Xaga 81 with Beatha 65
and Tine 70 (south-west), 6 Umiah (Umhrail) Lairmaster 81 (centre), 7 Giant Sporite Cluster 79 and
8 Easmarach 81 (the lake, centre-south), 9 Hurionthex 81 (far east), 10 Aroon the Urlamhai 81 (east
wing) with nine Sciath Drudges 75 numbered 1st-9th: Corp, Spiorad, Ropadh, Damna, Fuinneamh, Bru, Fuar,
Teas, Scor. The final room is a separate circle at the bottom reached by a long tunnel south from the
lake: 11-14 Guardian, Magical, Natural, Shadowy Earthmender 73; 15-18 Corrupt Primal of Air, Earth,
Fire, Water 83; 19 Olcasgean 87.
- Inference (L): the drudge names are Irish for damage types (body, spirit, thrust, matter, energy,
  crush, cold, heat, slash), matching the player report that each guard shields Aroon from one damage
  type.

**Dungeon rules.**
- Galladoria does **not** respawn on a timer. It resets only when the final encounter is cleared or on
  a patch/server reset (S4, S5; several posters, M-H). A "Galladoria Respawn Mob" in the Prima guide
  was guessed to be Olcasgean (S3, L).
- Made "markedly harder" in a mid-Dec 2002 patch: trash in threes, more bring-a-friend, Geomancer and
  Mechanism tougher (S3, Sloane; disputed by Unoid) (L-M).
- Clearing the dungeon took up to ~5 h; the final encounter up to 3 h (S6) (M).
- Whole dungeon except the final room doable with ~50 (S4, Unoid) (M).
- Usual kill order (S5, Vogar): Geomancer, Mechanism, Conservator, Broodmother, Xaga, Lairmaster,
  Hurionthex, Sporite Cluster, Easmarach; Aroon last or skipped. The Mechanism opens a wooden gate
  further in (S2) (M).
- Always clear the square-pathed hallways before entering the Mechanism area; coming straight from the
  Geomancer pulls every mob in them (S4) (M).

**Final room: Olcasgean** (S4, S5, S6).
- Reached by swimming left past the second waterfall, a hall of aggressive mobs, then a third
  waterfall with a narrow, hidden ledge; falling off or dying in the water means no rez (M).
- Entering triggers the whole room: when one raider crosses into the water at the waterfall, others
  may be pulled in too (S5, disputed) (L). Look straight at the waterfall when entering or you are
  dropped in the water (S6) (M).
- **Four Earthmenders** (Guardian, Magical/Magician, Natural/Naturalist, Shadowy/Stealth). Each can be
  damaged only by its matching Hibernian base class (Guardian = hero/champion/blademaster, Magician =
  mage classes, Naturalist = druid/bard/warden, Stealth = nightshade/ranger). Wrong-class damage heals
  it to full at once; they also cast heals on each other (slow casts, interruptible). S4, S5, S6 agree
  (H). Detail: a dev-raid player says vampiirs count as guardians and animists as magicians (L).
  Percival tests (S5): styles, procs, damage adds and heals between attackers are all fine if the class
  is right.
- **Four Corrupted Primals.** Earth (defends the menders; drag its aggro away up the ramp by the
  waterfall), Water (easy zerg), Air (hard to see; chain-casts AoE body mez "Tranquilize Area", body
  stun, attack-speed debuff, single 300 nukes; resisted a mentalist's whole power bar), Fire (moves on a
  fixed route crossing the platform, constant pulsing PBAoE, leaves "fire trail" mines that explode
  when stepped on; heals itself at ~20% and ~10%).
- Fire timing: US reports (Jan 2003) said it passed every ~9 minutes; EU (May 2003, patch 1.60)
  said it roams non-stop; Almisael says the 9-minute belief is what wiped them once (S5 vs S6;
  contradiction, likely a version difference; the EU version is the later one) (M).
- An "insane" water vortex / water elemental knocks people off the platform into the water (S3, S5)
  (M).
- **Winning plan (S6, first live kill, 7 May 2003, ~150-158 people incl. 15-20 void eldritches):**
  clear the dungeon; meet on the beach below the final tunnel; a nightshade enters to spawn the primals
  and watch Fire. Two safe spots exist, left and right of the entrance a little behind two of the
  healers, sheltered from fire trails. When Fire passes the entrance (best: when it heads north to the
  entrance then to the south-most spot, giving ~30 s), one group enters and tanks Water into the right
  safe spot; next pass, 6 more groups enter the right spot and hold Earth; next pass the rest go to the
  left spot. The left spot targets Fire and hits it with everything, mainly void eldritch GTAoE on a
  ground target placed on its flight path (60-100 per hit, ~20% landing); Fire died in 15-60 min.
  After Fire is dead the platform is safe: kill menders with the right classes, then zerg Earth and
  Water (~15-20 min), then Air (just shoot it ~15 min), then Olcasgean becomes aggressive and is an
  easy melee zerg. With 350-400 people and 40 void eldritches the week before they failed to kill one
  mender (chaos) (M).
- Alternatives (S5, L-M): mages kill Magical mender first (most dangerous to leave), then Guardian,
  Shadowy, Natural; order the primals Water, Earth, Fire, Air (dev-raid memory "maybe"); or Water,
  Earth, Fire, Air vs S6's Fire first. US trebuchet attempt on Fire failed (S6).
- Mythic patch 1.61 (EU, May 2003) added only a respec-stone chance to the final boss (S6, quoting EU
  notes) (L).
- Drops: Vine Choker needs a full Galladoria class suit (Herald, later); respec stones rare (H1347)
  (H).

Coverage: **good** for Olcasgean and the minis; numbers (HP, damage) thin.

## 6. Dragons

**Introduced** in 1.45 (test 29 Jan 2002, live 7 Feb 2002) with three new models: wolf-headed
(Gjalpinulva), stone (Golestandt), glimmer (Cuuldurach) (H177, H193; H). The 1.45 notes say the dragons
have special AI used depending on the situation (Mythic would not list it), and that once a day the
dragon gets restless and **patrols his whole zone**, announced by a zone-wide broadcast, swooping on
clearings, monster camps and busy paths; while patrolling he does not stay to fight but can be damaged
(H189, H193; H). Lore: Golestandt in Dartmoor's giant country; Gjalpinulva, daughter of Fenris, made
scaled two-legged wolves (Malmohus); Cuuldurach the Glimmer King rules the Sheeroe Hills (H177; H).

**Golestandt (Albion, Dartmoor)** (S1, M unless noted).
- Four successes with 125-160 people before realm abilities / epic armor; easier after the epic resist
  cut. 25 people killed him on Bors in Aug 2003 (S38, M); Drasticle's 15-24-person raids (S7).
- Patrols roughly hourly (Salaris' guess) and passes near the four entrances of his "keep"; clear the
  four corner giants then. Each entrance is at the middle of a side. Salaris put one group on the north
  side (decoy that eats the breath) and split the rest over the other three; north charges first.
- Breathes more at range, especially on the initial approach; casting, archery and procs do **not**
  cause breath (rumour denied); heat does not heal him but is weak. Fight under or behind him in melee
  range; keep moving.
- Throws players into the air (falling damage); teleports players around the zone (re-aggro on them
  can trigger breath). At low health a PBAoE stun of about 30 s on everyone in melee range (purge
  counters); calls giants for help: kill them first.
- Rewards: 4 x 100% dragon items, 12 Dartmoor items, 50 respec stones.
- Map (S21, L inference): Dartmoor's walled square with corner structures and a central dome at about
  40k, 60k in the southern rocky area matches Salaris' four-entrance "keep".

**Small-raid dragon method** (S7, Drasticle, Aug 2003, M; applies to all three, which he says act
almost identically):
- Ideal 24 people, at least 9 rezzers and one person with crack (purge? unclear); best defensive tanks
  (savages, sword-and-board paladins, s/b heroes); groups balanced with healers inside.
- The "glare": a targeted player must run away from everyone but **never leave the lair**; leaving the
  lair enrages the dragon into heavy AoE. Healers casting away from their protector cause the glare
  AoE; heal chant, procs and fire damage do not. Fixed AoEs at 90%, 75%, 40% and 20% health: scatter.
- Always kill adds first; one person on the dragon does nothing because he regenerates faster.
- No pets: if a pet is glared, both pet and owner can be AoE'd; no necromancers.
- Friars (or paladins twisting heat resist) help. 30-40 min with 24 (_Neysa_).
- Mythic later made old-world dragons resist raids of fewer than 3 groups (H1082/H1095, H).

**Gjalpinulva (Midgard, Malmohus)**: no fight description in the corpus beyond "almost identical" (S7)
and a Classes of Camelot guide title (S39, link lost). Lore H177. A 31 Jan 2003 Herald headline says
"Midgard's dragon was suffering a bit of a split personality" (read-file fix; text not saved)
(`2-patch-notes-20261113/www.camelotherald.com/8a5edab282632443_20030202170612.html`, L). Malmohus map
shows a ring structure about 20k, 55k (S21, L inference).

**Cuuldurach (Hibernia, Sheeroe Hills)**: called the hardest monster in the game by a 2003 commenter
(S30, L). The Stonehenge (EU) Hibernians had killed their dragon before their Olcasgean kill (S6, L). Sheeroe map shows a stone
ring at about 58k, 60k (S21, L inference).

**Xanxicar (Albion SI dragon, Crystal Cave under Avalon City)** (S1, M):
- Crystal Cave has ~11 mobs: ten Xanxicaran Elites (level 75+ "reavers" with reaver chants, massive
  damage; three near the entrance, the middle one roams) plus Xanxicar.
- Lightning dragon: very high energy resist, seems weak to spirit. Calls for help at ~50%. Throws far
  more than Golestandt. Breath is a PBAoE; he broadcasts that he stomps the ground first: run. Low melee
  damage; can be single-tanked if aggro is held. Pet spam essential near the end or he regenerates.
- "Get the loot and run" after the kill (a surprise follows, unspecified).
- Suggested well over 100 players. Drops: 2 ROG items only (later raised: Herald 1.65 "Nosdoden,
  Myrddraxis and Xanxicar will now drop more items", H1085/H1095; 1.68 "Xanxicar will now drop the
  appropriate amount", H1338) (H).
- Map (S17): a long curving entry tunnel into one big round cavern dotted with rock pillars (H).

Coverage: Golestandt **good**, small-raid method medium, Gjalpinulva and Cuuldurach **thin**, Xanxicar
medium.

## 7. Summoner's Hall and the old RvR dungeons

**Grand Summoner** (S1, M; Albion side, early 2003): 4-5 full groups (fewer possible since aggro holds
on one target). Changes form at ~35% health lost (mostly cosmetic). Parries (stand behind). Heals
himself; a few minor spells. Drops 93% items and the Silken Mantle cloak.
- Grand Summoner Govannon "now guaranteed to drop a magic item": 1.65a test 21 Aug 2003 (H1019), 1.65
  live (H1095) (H).
- Summoner Cunovinda dropping Albion gloves for Midgard players fixed, 1.66 (H1149, H1152) (H).
- Summoner Lossren's lair reachable by teleport on Gaheris/Mordred, 22 Sep 2004 (H1704) (H).
- Roesia: **no mention anywhere in the corpus**.

**History** (H): the three old RvR dungeons (Hall of the Corrupt, Doden's Gruva, Marfach Caverns) led
to Summoner's Hall. In 1.70j (30 Apr 2004) the Passage of Conflict replaced them; Summoner's Hall was
briefly unreachable (H1459). New Frontiers reworked the RvR dungeon into one dungeon plus a Summoner's
Hall zone (HNF). Monsters in PoC and Summoner's Hall drop realm respec stones (1.70, H1549). Clustering
(Dec 2005) shares DF and Summoner's Hall across servers (Herald more/1918).

**Hall of the Corrupt** (S1, M): ROG loot. **Archwizard**: about 2 groups; splits into weaker
duplicates; only the real one drops loot. Aidon and Gildas: **not named anywhere** in the corpus.

**Doden's Gruva** (S19 map, M): entrance in Breifine at 60k, 40k; zone lines to the Midgard frontier
(north-west) and to Summoner's Hall (south-east). Named spawns: Mistress of Runes (west), Hamer,
Aros the Spiritmaster, Jarl Ormarr "and friends" (east), Gudlaugr (dire wolf pit, south-east).
No fight descriptions.

**Marfach Caverns** (S20 map, M): entrance Jamtland Mountains 62k, 48k; two floors; zone wall to
Summoner's Hall on the lower level's west side. Named: Bas Altain (upper), Red Lady, Black Lady,
Blue Lady, Avartack the Champion, Chieftain Caimheul, Dark Eldritch, Ancient Black Oak. Patch: the
Blue Lady's summoned weapons no longer leave her room (1.69a, 9 Mar 2004, H1370) (H).

Coverage: **thin** (Grand Summoner only); Roesia, Aidon, Gildas **none**.

## 8. Krondon (Albion SI, Aldland)

**Map** (S16, M): entrances in Aldland at grid 26,5 (Outerward) and 9,6 (tunnel). Bosses: Morgus
Urgalorg and Beran the Supply Master (north), Scurceol Hyrde with the Four Lyft Miht (north-east
corner room), Fuladl (east), Fulafeallan (middle east), Deben se Gyende (west), Jehraq and Vlor se Bysen
(middle south), then a southern complex: Lurfos the Herald, Krevo Ricik, Drihten Elreden, and Orshom
Brond with Orylle at the very bottom.

**Mechanics** (S1, early 2003, M):
- **Scurceol Hyrde**: invulnerable while four stationary Nodes (the "Lyft Miht") live; nodes are high
  level and fight by throwing players into the air (use /stick to reduce falling damage). ~50 players
  for hit rate; pet spam. Battlegroup credit from 1.66h (H1197).
- **Orylle and Orshom Brong**: very strong; Orshom can be pulled and fought first. ~50+. Orshom keeps a
  fire lit; ice damage puts it out, which defangs Orylle, whose favourite attack is throwing players
  into the fire. Both parry. Orylle hits extremely hard (aggro control hard).
- Beran: ignites barrels so parts of the room explode. Easy.
- Morgus Urgalorg: ogre wizard training other wizards; no special tactic.
- Fuladl and Fulafeallan: blobs that split into smaller blobs; kill the small ones first.
- Deben se Gyende: commander with ogre fighters and mages; smart AI; relog-pull his guards first.
- Lurfos the Herald: adds fire and ice effects to his weapon.
- Ulor (map: Vlor) and Jehrog/Jehraq se Bysen: pair; Vlor casts, Jehraq melees; kill the caster first
  with pet spam.
- Krevo Ricik: unremarkable. Drihten Elreden: ogre friar with many styles; his students mostly watch.
- Patch: hall guard and patrol spawn rate "greatly reduced" to ease travel to the bosses, 1.68f
  (13 Feb 2004) / 1.68 (H1332, H1338) (H).

Coverage: **medium** (one source, but specific mechanics for every boss).

## 9. Avalon City (Albion SI)

Five Drakoran clan bosses (S1, M). Killing one clan's members earns faction with another, giving a safe
corner of the city.
- **Dra'argus the Mighty**: probably the hardest; invulnerable until the Orb upstairs in his building is
  destroyed; the Orb has a strong fire PBAoE; if the Orb dies and nobody attacks him quickly, he flees
  and despawns. 40-50+ players.
- Weno'iak the Enlightened: lightning effects; basically a straight fight.
- Sys'sro the Ruthless: easiest; one group hugging the wall; throws players into the pit in his area
  (rez from outside the pit).
- Dura'ek the Empowered: clear his adds except the last two; straight fight.
- Vera'erius the Brave: fight "honourably" (melee, buffs, heals only) and his adds stay out; anyone who
  retreats outside his tower or casts the wrong kind of spell brings powerful adds.
- Named drops got procs/charges in 1.68 (H1338) (H).

Coverage: **medium** (one source). No map of the city in the corpus (S22 shows only its location).

## 12. Open-world big bosses

- **Green Knight** (S1, M): on the Forest Sauvage zone border and moves across it during combat; about
  Grand Summoner numbers (4-5 groups) but more deaths: high melee, fast, styles; teleports often;
  summons trees (yellow to a 50); low heat AoE; heals himself; parries often. 93% loot.
- **Glacier Giant and Evern**: named by Salaris as Green Knight's equals (S1, L-M). Glacier Giant has a
  2-3 item static list (Classes of Camelot FAQ, `6-mechanics-20261113/www.classesofcamelot.com/7518de1b8e752a60_20050306170842.html`, H). Evern, his wraith drakes and wraith faeries "returned to Breifine"
  in 1.70t, 26 May 2004 (H1497) (H). No fight details.
- **Morgana**: lore only (Herald article id=109 "Discovery of the Dark Spire", Albion race pages); no
  encounter (as expected; Darkness Rising era).
- **Shrouded Isles named** (S1, M):
  - Colialt (Gwyddneau, Clifton): lich, about Legion difficulty; frees himself at midnight each night
    and attacks the Lammias; **invulnerable from midnight to 6:00 AM**, then a short kill window;
    keeping the Lammias alive (with faction) helps.
  - Self-Proclaimed King Dulgar: melee, 2 groups; awkward line of sight.
  - Valnir Modreth (Dales of Devwy): lich, 2+ groups; kill his essence ghouls first; lifetaps. Herald
    1.66q: blood liches and ancient zombies tied to the Valnir encounter no longer aggro when other
    undead nearby are hit (H1157) (H).
  - Ris'Nir Cruss: Drakoran; lightning PBAoE and occasional AoE mez; 2 groups.
  - Cabalist Meical: 1 group; parries; casts; room mobs help him.
  - Tower Keeper and Tower Guardian: easy, 95% loot.
  - Vortanos (Caldey): moderate lich, small group, 95% loot.
  - Amphipteres (Caldey): one per element, each with its own spells; a group each.
- **Albion classic epics** (S1, M): Moran the Mighty (1-2 groups; take the adds first), Callieach
  Urgaig (1-2 groups; calls for help low; four pillars shoot weak fireballs), Sister Blythe (700+
  hits), Llyn Barfog's six (Mouth, King of Barfog Hills, Fester, "Teaz--", Afanc with 1000+ melee,
  Wrath of Mordred who parries, double-hits and procs ~800 DD), Ellyl Lord (massive bring-a-friend).
  The Afanc arrived in 1.45 (H193).
