# Albion, Darkness Falls and shared bosses: web research notes

Compiled 2026-11-14 for the Living Server bot-raid work. Scope: Caer Sidi, Krondon, Avalon City and
Crystal Cave, Hall of the Corrupt, Golestandt, Summoner's Hall, Darkness Falls and Albion's open-world
bosses. Everything is summarized in my own words. Quotes are kept to a short phrase.

## How to read this

- **Period** means written 2001-2006 while the original mechanics were live. **Late** means 2007 or
  later: still live-server testimony, but after the 1.100/1.101 epic revamps (Dec 2009 / Feb 2010) or
  other reworks. **Secondary** means freeshard or wiki material.
- Confidence: **H** = official notes, or two or more independent period accounts that agree. **M** = one
  detailed period account. **L** = a single short or anecdotal comment, or a late or secondary source.
- Era warning: several encounters changed a lot. Govannon and the Summoner's Hall changed with New
  Frontiers (1.70, mid-2004). Golestandt and Xanxicar changed in 1.100/1.101. Apocalypse changed in
  1.58, 1.59 and 1.65, and again in 1.98 (2009). Pick one era per boss and don't mix them.

# CAER SIDI

## General dungeon rules (applies to every Caer Sidi boss)

- **Respawn:** The whole dungeon respawns at once, 8-10 hours after the first keylord (Skeletal
  Sacristan) dies, so a raid races the clock. S1 recommends 2-3 dedicated relog pullers. (M)
- **Spawns are triggered, not timed.** A puller who disbands, relogs and pulls with no heals or buffs
  on them gets one mob even from bring-a-friend (BAF) packs. A heal, a group invite or an early hit on
  the target breaks this. A dead puller who is not healed after the rez counts as relogged. Sources: S12
  for Caer Sidi specifically, S1 in general. (H)
- **Hard to hit:** Mobs are very hard to hit, especially with spells. S12 says the trash lacks the
  damage-add scaling that epic bosses have, so damage adds and damage shields hit Caer Sidi trash hard.
  It also implies that epic bosses scale those effects down. S12 calls for damage adds, damage shields
  and Pulsing Blade Turn (PBT). (M)
- **Hit chance rises with attacker count:** Each extra attacker, pets included, lowers a mob's
  defense, so pet spam (theurgists) and raid size raise everyone's hit rate. The high-level bosses have
  a practical attacker threshold below which they seem invincible (S1). Mythic's Feb 2004 Majestic Will
  change (local Herald capture) says epic kills had been rising fast because the to-hit bonus was
  larger than documented, which confirms that to-hit is the gating stat on epics. (M)
- **Parry:** Many bosses parry heavily from the front, so raids attacked from behind or the sides (S1,
  and the FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi advice" thread, 2003-03-26). In tight archways the main tank should
  back out into open space. (M)
- **Layout:** The dungeon is essentially a maze with ten locked doors. Named "key" mobs drop the keys,
  and raids went back and forth to find the right doors (FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi Raid:- Update and
  info", 2003-02-18). Apocalypse's door opens only after Lord Sanguis dies (late guide, see Sanguis).
  Each keylord area must be fully cleared first, or the whole floor comes in when the boss is pulled
  (S1; FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi Guide" thread, 2003-03-13). (M)
- **Raid sizes on live, 2003:**
  - 3 full groups (FG) for the early keylords.
  - 23 players killed Ilron.
  - About 40 to move at a decent pace.
  - 40-60 risk wiping at the Host or Bellum.
  - 80-90 could kill Apocalypse; 90-100 found him straightforward; 120+ "a walk in the park".
  - Sources: FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi Raid - Sunday 20th July", posts by Pin 2003-07-15/17,
    Flimgoblin, Ramas.
  - Before the 1.59 change, Apocalypse took about 70. After it, about 120 (FreddysHouse "Take...
    something or other", 2003-04-14). The lowest live kill S1 knew of was 105. (M)
- **Patch 1.61 (early 2003):** Named Caer Sidi mobs can drop single-line respec stones, and Caer Sidi
  jewelry got more imbue points (S10).
- **Loot:** One 100% item per keylord plus random drops (S1). Full lists, including the
  Apocalypse-only items, are in S13.

## Skeletal Sacristan (keylord), level 77

- **Mechanics:** Calls for help, then flees at high speed. (S1, M)
- **Tactics:** Clear his whole area first. Pin him between two groups so he keeps turning and gets
  stuck. Minimum 2 groups. (S1)
- His kill starts the 8-10 hour respawn clock (S1).
- Bot relevance: needs a "flee" behavior, plus blocking positions for two groups.

## Spectral Provisioner (keylord), level 77

- **Mechanics:** Fights back, but sometimes drops items into players' backpacks to encumber them, then
  runs off. (S1, M)
- **Tactics:** At least 3 groups; watch encumbrance. (S1)

## Lich Lord Ilron (keylord), level 70-79

- S12 lists "70, 79", so perhaps two forms or a level range.
- **Mechanics:** On engage he creates several duplicates that also attack. The real Ilron looks solid;
  the copies are ghostly. Killing the real one kills them all. He also casts AoE mez. (S1, M)
- **Tactics:**
  - Hold the raid at the central staircase while pullers bring in everything except the boss.
  - Everyone assists the leader's target, because picking the real one in a small room is hard.
  - Mez cures or immunity help.
  - 4-5 groups suggested (S1). In July 2003 one EU raid killed him with 23 players (FreddysHouse).
- The 2003 loot list names a ring "LLron's Ring of Shielding" (S13).

## Warlord Dorinakka (keylord), level 81, giant

- **Mechanics:** Straight melee. Hits hard, has a lot of HP and parries. S1 likens him to a harder
  Wrath of Mordred. (S1, M)
- **Tactics:** Attack from behind. An EU guild killed Dorinakka and the Silencer with "just under
  3fg" (FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi Guide", 2003-03-13). (M)

## Silencer (keylord), level 81, magical ooze

- **Mechanics:** Adapts to damage types. A player who uses the damage type he has adapted to is
  teleported into a nearby pit. (S1, M)
  - On one 2003 raid he ported half the force into the pit, which slowed it down (FreddysHouse, Pin,
    2003-07-17). (M)
  - Not documented: how fast he adapts, or how many damage types he tracks.
- **Tactics:** Clear the pit before engaging, so ported players land safely. His own damage is low;
  his high level is the obstacle. 4-5 groups. (S1)
- Bot relevance: track the damage type in use, rotate damage types, and walk ported bots back from
  the pit.

## Bane of Hope (keylord), level 81, crush

- **Mechanics:** Straight melee. When badly wounded he teleports elsewhere nearby and regenerates
  (S1, M). A 2012 Allakhazam comment says he teleports around the tunnels if not killed fast enough (L).
- **Tactics:** Keep players posted at the likely teleport spots. 3-4 groups. (S1)
- The teleport destinations are not documented.

## Lord Sanguis / Lich Lord Sanguis (keylord), levels 79 and 81

- **Mechanics:**
  - He starts as an archwizard ("Lord Sanguis", level 79). When that form dies, several adds spawn
    and he returns as the Lich Lord (level 81). (S1, M)
  - The Lich Lord parries. He is vulnerable to crush and thrust but strong against slash and most
    magic. (S1, M)
- **Tactics:**
  - At least 4 groups. (S1)
  - A late small-group guide (reposted 2010) says: clear the blood mages first, let the first form
    kill a pet, then the real Lord Sanguis spawns with one add. Tank him and the add separately. (L,
    late)
  - Killing Lord Sanguis unlocks the door to Apocalypse's room. Same late guide; it fits "ten locked
    doors". (M)

## Crypt Lord, level 81, slash

- **Mechanics:** Plain moderate melee, but huge add waves come if the area isn't fully cleared before
  he is aggroed. (S1, M)
- Clear the areas around the Crypt Lord, Soul Reckoner and the Host before pulling them (FreddysHouse,
  Pin, 2003-07-15). (H)

## Soul Reckoner, level 81

- **Mechanics:**
  - Surrounded by several "Reckoned Souls" (level 75), which must die before he can be hurt. (S1, M;
    a 2012 Allakhazam comment agrees, L)
  - The souls all attack at once. (Allakhazam 2012, L)
  - He heals on player kills, as Legion does, or has very high regeneration. (S1, M)
- **Tactics:** Clear the area, then kill the souls, then the boss. Keep players alive so he can't heal
  on kills.

## The Host, level 79, crush

- **Mechanics:** Several separate strong mobs that travel together. Killing any one kills them all.
  (S1, M)
  - S12 and Allakhazam list a single "Host" entry, so the group may be one mob plus linked bodies.
- **Tactics:**
  - Clear the whole area before aggroing it, because its aggro pulls in a lot. (S1)
  - A late guide (2010) says it patrols the stairs to Apocalypse's room with a large, buggy aggro
    radius. A reply says it is usually neutral and dies fast when pulled alone. (L, late)
  - Groups of 40-60 risk wiping at the Host (FreddysHouse, Pin, 2003-07-17). (M)
- Bot relevance: implement "kill one, all die": focus a single member of the Host, don't spread damage.

## The Four Horsemen (Fames, Bellum, Morbus, Funus), level 83 each; mounts are Fate's Bearer, level 77

- **Trigger:**
  - Walking into their room starts a speech, and you answer their question to begin (S1). A late guide
    says you talk to them in the middle of the room while the raid waits on the right wall. (M)
  - Each horseman is invulnerable for about a minute between fights, as a prep window. (S1, M; agrees
    with the 2010 guide)
  - A failed fight resets everything: the horse healed and the horses and horsemen went back to their
    start spots (FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi - Take 4", 2003-03-15). The encounter was buggy in spring and
    summer 2003. (M)
- **Fames (first):** Simple melee, the easiest one (S1). The 2010 guide says "two mobs", meaning the
  rider plus his mount (Fate's Bearer). (M)
- **Bellum (second, slash):** Immediately summons several animated weapons ("War Incarnate", level 75)
  that are very deadly. Kill Bellum fast and the weapons vanish (S1, M). The 2010 guide says the flying
  weapons share one HP pool and hit lightly, which reads like a later tuning (L). Plan on at least 6
  groups from Bellum onward (S1). Bellum wiped an early 2003 raid (FreddysHouse, 2003-03-13). (M)
- **Morbus (third):** Summons adds ("Swarm of Morbus", level 75, insects). Unlike Bellum's adds, these
  must die before Morbus can be damaged (S1, M). The 2010 guide says the adds cast disease, so take
  aggro first and then cure (L).
- **Funus (fourth):**
  - Only clerics can harm him. Any other class's attack instantly heals him to full. (S1, M; confirmed
    on FreddysHouse "Caer Sidi advice", 2003-03-31: raids shouted "clerics only", and it took about a
    minute to get non-clerics to stop.) (H)
  - The 2010 small-group guide says any damage other than a damage shield heals him, and has the necro
    pet attack the mount instead. That points to a late change or a damage-shield loophole. (L)
- **Loot:** Two 100% items per horseman. (S1)
- **Fate's Chosen** (level 81, "monster", crush) is listed in S12 and S14 near the horsemen. No source
  says what it does. It may be a horseman-encounter add or a Funus-phase add. Treat it as unknown.
- Bot relevance: Funus needs a class whitelist on attacks, with every non-cleric bot holding fire, pets
  included. Bellum needs a kill-the-boss priority; Morbus needs adds-first.

## Apocalypse (final boss), level 87, undead, slash

- **Mechanics, original 2003:**
  - At the start of the fight he flies up and fire rains from the ceiling for the rest of the battle
    (S1, H). Another EU account calls the hazards "raining fire and boiling earth" (FreddysHouse, Pin,
    2003-07-15). A Feb 2003 thread likens the ceiling fireballs to Golestandt's (FreddysHouse
    "Update and info"). (H)
  - **Harbingers of Fate** (level 75) spawn once players start dying. Pin and S1 both put the threshold
    at about 4 deaths, and both are unsure of the number.
    - S1 says four spawn in total, two at a time. Pin says each further death spawns more. These
      conflict.
    - Kill Harbingers before Apocalypse: they have much less HP and nearly his damage. (S1, M)
  - Players who move too far from him are teleported to the center of the room. (S1, M)
  - Parries, so attack from behind. Holding aggro on one tank is possible but unreliable. (S1) Pin
    says fully buffed defensive paladins could tank him, twisting heal chants. (M)
  - Melee hits are about 600-800 (late reply, 2010, L).
  - Apocalypse has his own loot table on top of normal Caer Sidi loot: the Warlord's Golden weapons, the
    Supple Warlord's Vest, and three necklaces that need a full Caer Sidi class suit to wear (S13).
- **Tactics, 2003:**
  - Raid size is the main factor: about 70 before 1.59, about 100-120 after. (S1, S15)
  - Keep everyone alive to avoid Harbingers. Pin leans on clerics and friars.
  - Pre-buff constitution, friar heat resist and the paladin's tri-element resist chant against the
    fire. (FreddysHouse, Pin, 2003-07-15)
  - S1 advises against dedicated rez groups at Apocalypse, because they cut the attacker count and a
    wipe can't be recovered anyway.
  - Use pet spam, AF debuffs, attacks from behind and aggro management. Don't run away.
- **Patch history:**
  - 1.58 (2003-01-07): Apocalypse's HP and resists lowered "a bit" (S8, H).
  - 1.59 (spring 2003): made significantly harder, from about 70 to about 120 people (FreddysHouse
    2003-03-27 and 2003-04-14; Mythic's note not found).
  - 1.65 (2003-10-08): difficulty tweaked "slightly downward" to match the other epic dungeons' final
    bosses (S3, H).
  - 1.98 (2009-06-05): he no longer calls the rest of the dungeon to help him (S14 description). That
    implies that before 1.98 his aggro pulled in the whole dungeon.
  - Late, post-revamp: a 2010 guide describes a room-wide AoE on his sword swing that instantly kills
    non-necro characters. That is a different encounter from 2003 (L, late).
- **Conflicts:**
  - Apocalypse's level: 83 (one player) against 87 (Prima). Prima is preferred.
  - Total Harbinger count: 4 (S1) against open-ended (Pin).

# KRONDON (Ogre Citadel, Shrouded Isles)

## General

- **Entry:** Ogres at the entrance were a problem early on. 1.61 added a side entrance dug by slimes
  (S10). 1.58 made the ogres inside Krondon aggressive to all intruders (S8). 1.61 put all ogres in the
  Followers of Orylle faction, aggressive to everyone (S10).
- **Hall traffic:** Hall guard and patrol spawn rates were "greatly reduced" in 1.68 (2004-02-19, S4).
  Before that, early-2003 raids hit "insane pops" and instant repops (FreddysHouse "Krondon, a nice
  place to visit", 2003-02-23; "SS alliance Krondon raid", 2003-03-27). (H)
- **Route, from Pin's 2003-03-27 post:**
  - West at the first fork leads to Deben se Gecynde.
  - East, then first left, past the jellies, leads to Fulafeallan.
  - South then north leads to Krevo Ricik.
  - The crossroads has Ulor and Jehrog to the west, Drihten to the east and Lurfos to the south.
  - A couple of corners past Lurfos are Orylle and Orshom.
  - Scurceol is on the other branch, with four more bosses along his path.
- **Levels:**
  - Lyft Miht 81, Orylle 70, Ulor 65, Morgus 60, Deben 55.
  - Most other named bosses are listed at 50 or "1-50" (S14). This looks like a placeholder; 2003
    players saw high purples.

## Scurceol Hyrde (Ogre's Might quest boss), levels 59/69/79/81/89/99; orbs/nodes are "Lyft Miht", level 81

- **Mechanics:**
  - Four nodes or rings (Lyft Miht) stand around him. While they are up he can't be damaged. (S1, M)
  - Each node adds 10 levels to him: with all four down he is level 59, with all four up 99. (froler-mid,
    FreddysHouse 2003-02-23; it matches the published 59-99 level list, so M-H)
  - The nodes are stationary. They "fight" by throwing players into the air for fall damage; /stick
    reduces the falls. (S1, M)
  - The nodes respawn. One group took him to about 50% when a ring repopped and made him untouchable
    again; on another try he healed back to 100% after the orbs repopped. (FreddysHouse, Bofgrey and
    SoulFly, 2003-02-24/25, M)
  - Many players died within seconds at the first ring. (Bofgrey, M)
- **Tactics:**
  - About 50 players (S1); about 40 (froler-mid).
  - Pet spam, because the nodes are high level.
  - Kill all four nodes, then burn Scurceol before the respawn. Bring enough damage to beat the node
    repop timer (Bofgrey suggests one more FG). (M)
- **Patches:**
  - 1.61: players who die still get quest credit if allies finish the kill (S10).
  - 1.66h (2003-11-13): Scurceol gives quest credit to the whole battlegroup (S5).
  - Early 2003: only the main aggro group got credit, a reported bug (froler-mid).
- **Loot:** About 2 items plus the quest belt (S1). Lyft Miht drops the "Spirit Core of the Node"
  (Allakhazam 2006).
- The node respawn timer is not documented anywhere I found.

## Orylle (ogre queen, "THE boss", level 70 giant) and Orshom Brong

- **Mechanics:**
  - Both are very strong and both parry. (S1)
  - Orshom keeps a fire burning ("Orshom's Fire", level 50 elemental). Orylle's most frequent attack is
    throwing players into that fire. (S1, M)
  - Ice (cold) damage can put out the fire, which makes Orylle much less dangerous. (S1, M)
  - Orylle's melee is "simply massive", so aggro control is hard. (S1)
  - 1.60 (2003-02-24) greatly reduced the spell effects in this encounter for frame rate, which
    suggests a lot of visual fire and spell activity (S9).
- **Tactics:**
  - Pull Orshom first and fight him apart from Orylle for a while (S1).
  - 50+ players (S1). Pin estimated at least 2 FG more than his 4+ FG (2003-03-27).
  - Two EU raids of about 80 players killed Orshom and Orylle in July 2003 (FreddysHouse "Ogre's Might
    & Krondon Raid", 2003-07-27).
- 1.64a: Orylle's unique drops now scale to the right levels (quoted on FreddysHouse, 2003-07-29).
- Bot relevance: assign cold-damage casters to the fire, kill Orshom first, and move bots out of the
  fire after a throw.

## Drihten Elreden (ogre friar)

- Uses many styles, and his strongest styles follow his evades and parries. High evade and parry, so
  flank him from behind. (S1; Pin 2003-03-27, M)
- His students stand by as neutrals, so pull him away from them. Adds from behind heal him if they
  aren't dealt with quickly. (Pin, M)
- About 5 minutes with a 4-FG raid. Dropped a 100% quality, AF 102 item (Flimgoblin, 2003-04-16).

## Deben se Gecynde ("Deben se Gyende"), level 55

- A military commander with ogre fighters and mages, and "excellent" AI. Relog-pull as many guards as
  possible first. (S1)
- 10-12 purple adds repop every 30-60 seconds, so kill them fast as they spawn. Hits softly, has high
  HP. Died in about 5 minutes to 4 FG. (Pin, 2003-03-27, M)

## Beran the Supply Master

- Ignites barrels around the room, and parts of the room explode for area damage. Not hard. (S1, M)
- Bot relevance: move bots off the burning barrel zones.

## Fuladl and Fulafeallan (named blobs)

- Each splits into smaller blobs when attacked. The main blob is very defensive, so kill the adds
  first (S1).
- Pin says Fulafeallan's small blobs die quickly. The main blob's HP bar went past 100% and took time
  to bring back under, then dropped fast. It went invulnerable ("IP'd") about twice. (2003-03-27, M)

## Lurfos the Herald

- A strong melee fighter who puts fire and ice effects on his weapon. (S1)
- His area had constant instant-pop purple adds, which wiped Pin's raid. (2003-03-27, M)

## Ulor and Jehrog se Bysen (Ulor level 65)

- Fight together: Ulor casts offensive magic, Jehrog melees. Pet spam and kill Ulor first, then
  Jehrog. (S1, and Pin 2003-03-27; H)
- Each died in about 10 seconds to 4 FG; 1-2 groups are enough (Pin). Clear the adds, which repop
  fast.

## Krevo Ricik

- Nothing notable (S1). Has 1-2 adds; one group is enough (Pin). (M)

## Morgus Urgalorg (level 60)

- An ogre wizard training other ogre wizards. No special tactics (S1). Allakhazam lists "Morgus's
  Leornere" (level 60) as his students. (L)

# AVALON CITY AND CRYSTAL CAVE

## Avalon City bosses (one per drakoran clan)

- **Dra'argus the Mighty** (level 68):
  - Invulnerable until the powerful orb upstairs is destroyed. The orb is the real danger, with a
    strong fire PBAoE. (S1, M)
  - If you kill the orb and don't attack him quickly, he flees and disappears. (S1)
  - 40-50+ players. (S1)
  - The Eden freeshard version: tank him downstairs while a team kills the "Dray'gyn Sphere" upstairs,
    then everyone returns. (S17, secondary)
- **Weno'iak the Enlightened:** Extra lightning damage effects; otherwise a straight fight (S1). On Eden,
  pull the adds from the courtyards first and the boss last (S17).
- **Sys'sro the Ruthless:** The easiest. One solid group hugging the wall. He throws players into the
  pit in his area, so rez from outside the pit. Clear his area first. (S1, M)
- **Dura'ek the Empowered:** Clear his adds beforehand except the last two; otherwise straight. (S1)
- **Vera'erius the Brave:**
  - Fight "honorably" as he asks (melee, buffs and heals only) and his adds don't join. (S1, M)
  - Casting the wrong spell type, or anyone leaving his tower, brings several strong adds. (S1, M)
- 1.61 fixed monster pathing in Avalon City (S10).

## Crystal Cave trash: Xanxicarian elites, champions and sentries

## Xanxicar (Albion SI dragon), level 80 (lowered in 1.101)

  - A lightning dragon with high energy resists. S1 found him weak to spirit damage (smite). (S1, M)

  - **Breath/stomp:** S1 calls it a PBAoE that he announces ("stomping the ground") just before it

  - 1.68 / 1.68a (Jan-Feb 2004): drops the right number of items (S6, S4).

# HALL OF THE CORRUPT AND SUMMONER'S HALL

Era note: before New Frontiers (2001 to mid-2004), the Hall of the Corrupt was Albion's frontier
dungeon leading to Summoner's Hall. In 1.70 (NF, about July 2004), the Passage of Conflict replaced the
three frontier dungeons (local Herald patch notes), and the Summoner's Hall encounters were reworked.

## Aidon the Archwizard (Hall of the Corrupt, later listed in Summoner's Hall), level 65-70

- **Original:** About 2 groups. During the fight he splits into weaker duplicates; only the real one
  drops loot (S1, M). Four clones with hard-hitting nukes: mez the clones and focus the real one
  (FreddysHouse "The Halls of the Corrupt", 2003-04-29, M).
- **Later:** An "easy zerg"; splits into four (ToT 2006, S18). On Eden: tank him between the four
  pillars in his room or he resets, and keep interrupting the four adds (S17, secondary).

## Lord Gildas (Hall of the Corrupt), level 55-60

- Purple to level 50s and "very very hard". About as hard as Aidon. Blocks and parries heavily, so he
  is hard to hit. (Allakhazam description, about 2003, L)
- **Position:** The bottom of a pit like the scaled-fiend pit in Barrows, visible from the left side of
  the bridge. (L)
- Nothing on adds or special abilities.

## Grand Summoner Govannon (Summoner's Hall), level 80, demon

- **Original, 2002-2003:**
  - Three forms: an elf caster, then taller after losing some life, then a true demon form at about
    60%. S1 puts the change at about 35% HP lost and calls it cosmetic. (Allakhazam description; S1; M)
  - Parries and evades heavily from the front, so attack from behind or the sides. (S1; Allakhazam
    2002-10-22 and 2003-06-28; H)
  - Self-heals and casts minor spells. (S1)
  - Summoning his two minions once caused a wipe. (2003-06-28, L)
  - A room of adds aggroes during the fight if it isn't handled. (Allakhazam description)
  - Aggro can be held on one target, so small groups can kill him slowly.
  - Kills: 43 people in about 30 minutes; 14 people in 25-30 minutes; one group plus 2 buff bots in 45
    minutes; one full group in about 25 minutes (2003-2004). S1 suggests 4-5 FG.
  - Recommended group: paladin tank, armsman guard, earth theurgist (PBT), 2-3 clerics, a
    sorcerer/minstrel for power, extra damage (FreddysHouse 2003-04-29).
- **New Frontiers rework (July 2004 on):**
  - Three nodes before the fight: Healing, Fire and Shielding. Killing the Shielding node made him
    effectively unkillable (1 damage per hit), a bug that persisted in 2005. Kill only the Fire and
    Healing nodes. (Allakhazam 2004-07-17, 2005-05-10, 2005-09-09; M)
  - Two summoning orbs on the far side: one summons sacrifices, the other turns them into demons. PBAoE
    clears them. (2004)
  - About 5 skilled players minimum.
  - ToT, 2006: one shield tank and one endurance class hold him while the raid clears the orbs and
    nodes (back, then right, then left), then everyone burns him. (S18)
  - Eden: tank him on his pad; adds come at 75% and 35%. (S17)
- 1.65a/1.65 (Aug-Oct 2003): guaranteed to drop a magic item (S3).

## Summoner Roesia, level 55 ("Alb summoner")

- **2003:** Casts an uninterruptible self-heal (about 15% HoT) at 10-15% HP, which can stall the fight
  (FreddysHouse "Summoner Cunovinda", 2003-12-09, which calls her "Roena"). One fighter group died to
  her in about a minute. Called the hardest by one poster and the middle one by another. Pops purple
  adds (FreddysHouse 2003-04-29). (M)
- **2006 version:** She stays in the room's center with nodes along the walls. While the orbs are up
  she casts a forceful zephyr that lifts raiders into floating clouds. Kill all the orbs, then melee
  her. (S18)
- Eden: just burn her (S17).

## Summoner Cunovinda, level 60 ("Mid summoner")

- **2003:** A nuke followed by a toss into the air for fall damage (up to 75%), causing deaths that
  can't be avoided. Rez sickness stacks, making it a fight of attrition. Lowest HP and AF of the three.
  (FreddysHouse 2003-12-08/09, M)
  - Posters disagree on which summoner tosses: one says the Alb one does, another the Hib one.
- **2006:** Throws raiders for about 90% of their HP. Self-heals. Can teleport raiders to their last
  bind point. Raids often skipped her by porting past (S18).
- Eden: roots and avoids the four Massive Hulks she spawns, then burns her (S17).
- 1.66b (2003-10-30): no longer drops Albion gloves to Midgard players (S7).

## Summoner Lossren, level 75 ("Hib summoner")

- **2003:** Stands still and spawns orange conjured mobs that die in one hit, or strong yellow zombies a
  paladin can one-shot. Called the easiest (FreddysHouse 2003-12). (M)
- **2006:** Summons "tortured souls" for casters to kill; all melee on Lossren (S18).
- Eden: everyone in the small alcove behind him; no AoE damage in the room; AoE-mez the adds (S17).

# GOLESTANDT (Albion mainland dragon, Dartmoor), level 80

- **Layout:**
  - A "keep" with four entrances, one centered on each side, and a giant in each of the four corners
    (S1).
  - Prima's Dartmoor map marks it as "Golestandt & Castle Grounds" (S11).
  - Tethered to his castle (FreddysHouse 2008).
- **Mechanics, original 2002-2003, from S1:**
  - Patrols about once an hour (S1's guess), passing near several entrances. Clear the corner giants
    during that window.
  - **Breath:** Used most often on the initial approach and against targets at range. Spellcasting
    and archery do not trigger it; that is a myth. Constant movement reduces breath damage.
    - Patch 1.60 (2003-02-24) added a heat-resist debuff to the breath (S9, H).
  - Throws players into the air for fall damage.
  - Teleports players to nearby parts of the zone. Their return can draw another breath.
  - At low HP, a PBAoE stun on everyone in melee range for about 30 seconds; Purge counters it.
  - At low HP he calls for help, including giants. Kill the giants first: they hit hard and die faster.
  - Heat doesn't heal him but isn't effective either.
- **Mechanics, 2006-2008 accounts:**
  - "Golestandt glares at [name]" warns that player to run away from the group. (Allakhazam,
    Masjaun 2006-03-08, M)
  - Casting from outside melee range triggers an AoE fire blast, so everyone, healers and casters
    included, stays in melee range (same post). This conflicts with S1's 2003 claim that casting
    doesn't matter; it may be a later change.
  - At 50%, 3-4 adds spawn and go for the healers (2008).
  - Strips random buffs, especially constitution; silence; turns players into small stone giants (an
    uncurable disease and slow, removed by Purge); breath for about 2k (FreddysHouse 2008).
  - Summons the Hibernia and Midgard dragons, which die fast and drop only coin, or the adolescent
    dragons Cuuldurach and Gjalpinulva at 25-50% HP (Allakhazam 2007; FreddysHouse 2008). Their spawn
    rules conflict between US and EU servers.
- **Tactics, original:**
  - 125-160 players, plus relics on one raid (S1, early 2003).
  - One group charges from the north as a decoy to soak aggro and the first breath. The rest enter
    evenly through the other three entrances, after the giants are cleared.
  - Fight under or behind him in melee range. Pet spam for hit rate and extra targets. Rez groups and
    debuffs.
- **Tactics, later:**
  - 25 players by 2003-2004 (classesofcamelot news, local capture).
  - 23 players in 2.5 hours on a classic server (2006): two paladins hold aggro, cross-guard and twist
    heal chants. Theurgist pets lower his AF, but kill your own pets below 5% because a pet killing
    blow gives no loot.
  - An 8-man 2008 method: an armsman on a fixed spot about 2k out, clerics at maximum heal range
    (about 2.2k), scouts to the side and under him to avoid the cone, adds intercepted by scouts.
- **Patches:**
  - 1.52 (2002): "adjusted" for a fair, challenging fight.
  - 1.58 (2003-01-07): drops 50 Luminescent (respec) stones.
  - 1.60: heat debuff on the breath.
  - 1.100 (2009-12-15): harder; moved back inside his lair; corpse decays in 30 minutes, so Gerrik's
    Sword can force spawns.
  - 1.101 (2010-02-24): damage reduced; the breath grants an immunity timer against further resist
    debuffs; main specials' reuse raised from 2 to 5 minutes; mechanics added against inattentive
    players. (S14 descriptions)
- **Loot:** Four 100% "dragon items", twelve normal Dartmoor items and 50 respec stones (S1).

# DARKNESS FALLS (shared, all realms)

## Layout and access (S11 map key, pp. 358-359)

- Each realm has its own wing, entrance (Midgard #1, Hibernia #47, Albion #80), stores and portals.
- **Princes:**
  - Prince Abdin #20, near the Midgard entrance.
  - Prince Asmoien #35.
  - Prince Ba'alorien #71, near the Albion side.
  - Princess Nahemah #28.
- The Center is #81. Gatekeeper Dommel (#84) holds the Gate Room (#83) to the lower level.
- **High Lords (lower level):**
  - Oro #89, Baelerdoth #91, Saeor #93, "Bain" #98 (= Baln).
  - Each sits in a room with Dukes, Marquises and Earls.
- **End:**
  - Grand Chancellor Adremal #103, guarding the way to Legion.
  - #104 holds the Chamberlain with guards (Pale Guardian, Chaosian, Marquis Haurian, Inquisitors,
    Mutilators, Chthonic Knights, Umbrood Warrior) and Behemoth.
  - Legion #105.
- **Access:**
  - In 2002: only the realm holding the most frontier keeps could use its portals (wiki summary of the
    1.49 notes).
  - From 1.74: the realm holding the most towers; ties change nothing (S16, 2008).
  - Everyone inside can stay if access flips, as long as they don't release.

## Princess Nahemah, level 71-76

- Periodically summons skeletal minions that use a fire AoE (2003-06-12, M).
- Her STT ("shield of the target"?) makes pets attack their owner instead of her, so avoid pet pulls
  (Allakhazam, Scholar 2006-08-04). She also casts banshee bolts and conical AoEs. (M)
- **Respawn:** About every 90 minutes, or random (20 minutes once, 5 hours another time). (2003, L)
- **Group:** 3-6 players in 10-13 minutes (2002-2003).
- Part of the Midgard quest "Strange Bedfellows".

## Princes Abdin, Asmoien and Ba'alorien, level 78 each

- **S1 (early 2003):**
  - "Albion prince": a fairly straight fight, but relog-pull to avoid lots of adds. 1-3 groups.
  - "Midgard prince": uses pets, which can kill a main tank when everything focuses the same target.
    About 2 groups. His bridge is the easiest but repops fastest.
  - "Hibernia prince": the hardest bridge area, with two paths to him that can split a raid.
  - Princes drop about 3 items plus seals.
  - Which prince is which is unclear; S1 uses realm labels only. By the S11 map, Abdin is in the
    Midgard wing and Ba'alorien in the Albion wing.
- **Asmoien:** A large AoE or PBAoE self-heal (81% back to 100% in one cast). Anti-pet bolts fired
  through the gate killed Animist shrooms (Allakhazam, Scholar 2006-08-04, M). A 2015 comment thought
  he heals on kills instead (L).
- **Ba'alorien:** Heals himself each time he kills something; pets feed that, so remove them
  (Allakhazam 2006-04-17, M). Seven players or two FG are enough (2002-2003).
- 1.60 (2003-02-24): Asmoien's and Ba'alorien's AI updated "to make their encounters more reliable"
  (S9, H).

## High Lords Oro, Baln, Saeor and Baelerdoth (level 65-80)

- **"High Lord A" (S1 thinks "Iro"; likely Oro):** Two illusory duplicates that do damage but drop
  nothing and are much weaker. About as hard as a prince. (S1, M)
- **"High Lord B":** A standard fight with nothing special (S1). Probably Baelerdoth; my inference.
  - 2006 Allakhazam (Scholar): near the end of the fight he "summons Darkness Falls itself", with heavy
    AoE hits and a dragon-breath icon. He also has a pulsing PBAoE "absorb" debuff. An 8-player group
    killed him. (M, late-period)
  - 1.5 FG with the room cleared first was easy in 2003 (L).
- **Baln:** A big demon, a bit harder than the first two, with an occasional PBAoE. (S1, M)
- **Saeor:** A vampire type that attacks fast and hard and regains life when he kills a player. The
  hardest High Lord. (S1, M)
- High Lords drop about 2 items plus seals (S1).

## Beliathan (the worm), level 70 (earlier higher)

- **Spawn:**
  - Rare and random (official, S16 Grab Bag 2009-01-16).
  - Announced by a zone-wide rumble message ("halls ... rumble", "walls rumble and shake").
  - Players long believed killing all princes triggered him, which is false.
  - Despawns after a while. (FreddysHouse 2003, 2004; H)
- **Lair:** A small room with an ooze puddle where he couldn't be hit; pulled into the hallway he was
  killable (Allakhazam, Demi 2002-06-14, L).
- **Mechanics:**
  - Originally his level was set far above the rest of his difficulty, so small raids found him
    "invincible" and big ones found him easy. His level was lowered by early 2003. (S1, M)
  - Spawns small "rotting maggot" adds; per a developer, repeat summons aren't meant to drop loot.
  - Hits around 600-990 (2002-2004).
  - An AoE stun (2015, L).
- **Group:** 4-5 groups after the level cut (S1). 1-2 FG by 2004.
- The 2003 seal-farming exploit on maggots led to bans (FreddysHouse "FARM Beliathan").

## Grand Chancellor Adremal, level 79

- A standard melee boss with prince-quality loot, guarding the way to Legion (S1).
- He buffs his whole room, and a bad pull brings the entire room. If he isn't killed, his mutilators and
  knights stay dangerous (Allakhazam 2002-10-11, L/M).

## Legion (final boss), level 83, demon

- **Official, 2002-04-11 (S2):** Mythic found Legion "acted like such a cream puff" at launch because
  his specials weren't firing, and fixed him. After the fix:
  - He resists damage types appropriately.
  - He reclaims souls more often.
  - He can now target people far away from him.
  - Behemoth now stays in his own area; Legion had been summoning him from elsewhere.
  - The Chamberlain's helper now assists the Chamberlain.
- **Mechanics, 2002-2004 player accounts (H where they agree):**
  - Melee up to about 800 (S1). One-shot a solo minstrel (2002).
  - Heals himself when he kills a player ("consumes bodies"). This is why the tank must hold him and
    the raid must keep deaths down. (S1; FreddysHouse 2004; H)
  - Randomly teleports a group of players into Behemoth's chamber. They must run across the room to
    the portal on the far side, which returns them to Legion (S1). Run west (Allakhazam 2002-10-21;
    FreddysHouse 2004). Endurance buffs help the sprint. You can't choose who is ported (2006).
  - In Behemoth's room, clinging monsters ("clinging souls", corpses rising from the floor) root
    players. (S1; FreddysHouse 2004; H)
  - Adds:
    - Kill the add first (2002).
    - Adds popped at half his HP (2003).
    - A Chamberlain respawn ruined one attempt (2004).
    - Clear the room fully, then engage at once to avoid an early respawn. If a respawn happens, kill
      the lesser mobs first, since Legion himself is easy to keep on one target. (S1)
- **Tactics:**
  - One group takes his aggro onto the main tank as soon as the room is clear.
  - About 50 players for the attacker-count bonus, though 3 groups can do it slowly. (S1)
  - Reports range from 3-4 FG to 6-7 FG, and one 100-player kill (FreddysHouse 2004).
  - A Midgard recipe: Purity, augmented shamans, damage adds, L50 mending; a third to a half of the
    effort of the dragon (Allakhazam, Scholar 2003-04-29).
  - Use theurgist pet spam, debuffs, rez groups, shield tanks with PBT, and disease to cut his
    life-tap.
- **Patches:** Credit to the entire battlegroup (1.66h-era note, local capture). Titles in 1.82.
- **Loot:** Four 100% items, eight prince-quality items and about 500 seals (S1, unsure of the count).

## Behemoth, level 99, Legion's teleport room

- **Not killable.** No loot. You can only get there by being sent. (S16 Grab Bag 2008-01-11, official,
  H)
- Hits for 2,000-3,000 (Allakhazam description, and several 2002 posts; H).
- Roams the room ported players land in and slowly closes on anyone who lingers (2002).
- A 2006 account says he is immune to magic and evades all melee; players can't even enter combat with
  him (FreddysHouse 2006).
- The Plated Fiend "weakening" rumor was disputed and never confirmed.
- A 2009 report says his HP fell to about 35% and then snapped back to 60-70% several times. That is
  late and probably an invulnerability or reset.
- In April 2002 Legion pulled him in from elsewhere by mistake; that was fixed (S2).
- Bot relevance: ported bots should never engage. Path to the far portal, which is west; cure or
  ignore roots.

# Contradictions to resolve (summary)

1. **Apocalypse's Harbingers:** S1 says four in total, two at a time. Pin says each death after about
   4 spawns more. The death threshold itself is uncertain (about 4) in both.
2. **Apocalypse's level:** 83 (one player) against 87 (Prima, Allakhazam). Use 87.
3. **Funus:** Cleric-only damage, with others healing him to full (2003, multiple sources), against
   "anything but a damage shield heals him" (2010 small-group guide). Use the 2003 rule for 2003.
4. **Xanxicar's breath:** A telegraphed PBAoE stomp (S1, 2003) against a cone you can dodge by standing
   behind him (2006).
5. **Xanxicar and pets:** Pet spam is near essential (S1) against an energy shield that kills pets fast
   (2006).
6. **Golestandt and range:** Casting or archery doesn't matter, though the breath favors far targets
   (S1, 2003), against casting outside melee range triggering a fire AoE (2006). Possibly a patch in
   between.
7. **Beliathan's spawn:** "Kill all princes" (player lore) against random (official 2009). Use random,
   with the rumble message.
8. **Bellum's weapons:** "Extremely deadly, kill Bellum" (S1, 2003) against "shared HP, light hits"
   (2010).
9. **Which Summoner's Hall summoner tosses players:** 2003 posters disagree (Alb, Mid or Hib).
10. **Legion raid size:** 3 FG to 100 people. It depends on era and gear; S1 recommends about 50.
